About these files

  • Each image is the archived copy held in the campaign folder, shown at full size and linked to the file itself.
  • Nothing here is redrawn from memory. Where the table has no map, the page has no map.
  • Maps are player-facing: they show what the party has been given, not what the referee has behind the screen.
