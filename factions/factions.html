In detail

The Literators Guild or scholarly faction

Stance: hostile. Status: active. First seen: Session 1.

A guild of literators whose seat is the library, in the north of the city. Kollaro said so at the handover, which makes it the party's knowledge rather than a guess. They sent crossbowmen to kill Kollaro; the party killed two of them and took Mahir alive.

  • Opposed to Kollaro and the Barbers' guild.
  • Sent an armed ambush after Kollaro.
  • Mahir, their man in the ambush, was captured and handed to the barbers. Kollaro named him at the handover.
  • Their seat is the library in the north of the city, as Kollaro described it. Their motive is still unknown.
  • Nobody in the party has been to the library. What they know of it comes from Kollaro.

The Barbers' Guild Guild

Stance: wary; they used the party. Status: active. First seen: Session 1.

A powerful guild in Arakay. Kollaro is a member, and the party delivered his captured literator enemy to the guild's territory at Barber's Alley.

  • Kollaro is a member of the guild.
  • The guild controls Barber's Alley.
  • They took custody of the captured literator.
  • They are in conflict with the Literators.
  • Hiring the party through a false name, and then asking for a murder, is how they came to use them.

The Khosurans Army or polity

Stance: unknown. Status: at war. First seen: Session 1, by rumour.

According to Wafik, the Khosuran armies are fighting a war with some Easterners, and winning. The party has had no direct contact with them. Khosura is the purple city to the east, where the caravan was bound when it was destroyed.

  • The Khosurans are at war with Easterners.
  • Wafik says they are winning.
  • The war is background pressure in Arakay, not yet a direct threat.

The Pillar Worshippers Street cult or shrine congregation

Stance: unknown. Status: unknown. First seen: Session 1.

People worshipping at the broken white pillar, which is carved with a kneeling calf wearing a crown of leaves. Offerings surround the pillar. They did not invite questions, and none of the party asked one.

  • They worship at the broken pillar.
  • The pillar is white stone, possibly andesite or marble.
  • The carving shows a kneeling calf with a leaf crown.
  • Offerings are left there.

What is unresolved

Questions the archive keeps open rather than answering. Each is marked [?] on the faction's own page.

  • The Barbers' Guild: who leads it, what a barber's guild actually controls, and whether they killed Mahir after the party left.
  • The Khosurans: where the war is being fought, how it touches Arakay, and whether Wafik is repeating common talk or speaking from knowledge.
