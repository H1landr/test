# The Khosurans

**Type:** Army / polity  
**Status:** At war  
**First seen:** Session 1, by rumor

## Summary

According to Wafik, the Khosuran armies are fighting a war with some Easterners and are winning. The party has no direct contact with them. Khosura is the purple city to the east, the destination the party was bound for when the caravan was destroyed.

## What the party knows

- The Khosurans are at war with Easterners.
- Wafik says they are winning.
- The war is background pressure in Arakay, not yet a direct threat.
