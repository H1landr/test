# The Literators

**Type:** Guild or scholarly faction  
**Status:** Active  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A guild of literators whose seat is the library, in the north of the city. Kollaro said as much while he talked at the handover, which makes it the party's knowledge and not just a guess. They sent crossbowmen to kill Kollaro. The party killed two and captured Mahir.

## What the party knows

- Opposed to Kollaro and the Barbers' guild.
- Sent an armed ambush after Kollaro.
- **Mahir**, their man in the ambush, was captured and handed to the barbers. Kollaro named him at the handover.
- Their seat is the library in the north of the city, as Kollaro described it. Their motive is still unknown.
- What the party knows about the seat comes from Kollaro, not from a visit. Nobody has been to the library.
