# Two Clay Tablets — Divine Scrolls

**Type:** Divine magic scrolls
**Holder:** Illiterate Ed
**Status:** Carried, unused
**First seen:** Session 2

## Description

Two clay tablets taken from the hollow compartment of a stone altar in the undercroft, wrapped in a small bundle with five rough-cut amber gems.

- Tablet one: **Bless**.
- Tablet two: **Speak with Animals**.

They are divine magic, and neither sorcerers nor magic users can use them. Nobody in the party can cast them.

## Value

- **250 XP each if sold or traded**, divided among the party. The XP is independent of the sale price.
- No XP is awarded for finding them or for keeping them.
- No purchase price or market value in coin is recorded. The sale price is whatever a buyer will pay, and it does not change the XP.
- Full rule: [Magic item XP](../rules/magic-item-xp.md).

## History

- Found in the altar of the 15 × 15 ft room, Session 2.
- The compartment was trapped with a poison needle. Ed was struck and saved; it did him no harm.
