# Green Rod of Glowing Metal

**Type:** Unknown metal rod; magic or alchemical  
**Current holder:** The Chül, if still unsold  
**Status:** Seen and priced; not bought  
**Price:** Not recorded  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A rod of green metal among the Chül's wares, laid out on a blanket in the street. It gives off its own light, and daylight does not put it out. The Chül says chips taken from it will sharpen a spell. The party did not buy it and nobody touched it.

## Known facts

- Seen on the Chül's blanket in Session 1.
- Made of a metal that glows green, constantly, in daylight.
- The Chül claims chips of it sharpen a spell.
- Not purchased. No price was recorded.
- Neither the metal nor the claim has been tested.
