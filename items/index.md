## Index rules

- Track significant items individually; use [the treasure ledger](../state/treasure-ledger.md) for bulk coin and ordinary valuables.
- Record whether an item's powers are confirmed, reported, or unknown.
- If an item is sold or lost, keep the page and update its status.
