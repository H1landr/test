# Lapis-look Necklace

**Type:** Jewellery  
**Current holder:** The Chül, if still unsold  
**Status:** Seen and priced; not bought  
**Price:** 150 gp  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A necklace among the Chül's wares that had the look of lapis lazuli and carried a cat charm, priced at 150 gp. Nobody in the party touched it, and the price ended the conversation.

## Known facts

- Seen on the Chül's blanket in Session 1.
- Looks like lapis lazuli. Whether the stones are real is not established.
- Carries a cat charm. What the charm is made of is not recorded.
- Asking price: **150 gp**.
- Not purchased. After lodging, each purse held 14 gp, less the cakes; the necklace cost more than the party could raise.
