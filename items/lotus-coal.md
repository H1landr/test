# Lotus Coal

**Type:** Drug, charcoal
**Holder:** Illiterate Ed
**Status:** Carried
**First seen:** Session 2

## Description

Charcoal sold by the black-robed dealers at the stairs. It is breathed in, not eaten, and produces a transcendent experience. Ed bought a quantity for 3 gp after haggling from 5 gp, described at the time as half price.

## What the party knows

- Charcoal is a drug, not fuel, to these dealers.
- A sick-looking customer bought some and ran off.
- Ed has not used it.
