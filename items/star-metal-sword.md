# The Star-Metal Sword

**Type:** Sword, star metal
**Holder:** Orlando, somewhere below
**Status:** Missing; not wanted back by his mother
**First seen:** Session 2 (described)

## Description

A blade of star metal, carried by Lady Danarose's late husband during his service in the Khosurian military.

Orlando took it down with him to impress Pablo and to fight the Splinter. His mother does not want it back, and believes its being down there was part of Orlando's problem.

## What the party knows

- It is in the tunnels with Orlando, unless someone has taken it.
- Lady Danarose will not ask for its return, so recovering it would not breach the contract.
- Orlando's drawing shows a figure holding a curved sword forward.
