# Deities

The cultures document names chief deities but does not give full pantheons, domains, alignments, or clergy rules.

| Deity | Associated people | Known sphere or title | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Azura the Eternal Flame** | H'Zhari | Chief deity | Common Peoples and Cultures v1.1 |
| **Mitra** | Hyloran | Sun god; chief of the pantheon | Common Peoples and Cultures v1.1 |
| **Kleosiros the Red Lion** | Sarossian | Chief of the hero-gods | Common Peoples and Cultures v1.1 |
| **Bahemon** | Kythari | Mysterious dragon god; chief deity | Common Peoples and Cultures v1.1 |
| **Leyllwynll** | Essanin | Ancestor god | Common Peoples and Cultures v1.1 |
| **Kharnigh Kharl** | Ahzghalid | First dwarf; King Under the Mountain | Common Peoples and Cultures v1.1 |

## What is not established

- Domains, portfolios, alignment, and holy symbols.
- Temple organization, clergy titles, and liturgical calendars.
- Which gods are worshipped in Arakay.
- Whether the gods are real, mythic, or both.
- How cleric spellcasting relates to these deities.
