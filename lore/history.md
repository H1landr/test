# History

The supplied cultures document gives a broad chronology in relative terms. No absolute campaign dates are provided yet.

## Before the known empires

- **Kythar** existed on its great plateau before Anduir, before the H'Zhari, and before Acheron.
- The Kythari studied the heavens and pursued esoteric knowledge; their astrologers are said to rival the elves.

## The Acheronean Empire

- Acheron was a demon-haunted empire.
- The Sabbateans were bonded servants of Acheron and made up the bulk of its armies.
- Acheronean power spread fire and death through Jorvarah.

## The H'Zhari ascendancy

- Over a thousand years ago, the great cavalry armies of the H'Zhari overthrew Acheron.
- They came to rule all of Northern and Eastern Jorvarah.
- Over centuries, their unified dominion fractured into smaller kingdoms and city-states.

## The kingdom of Anduir

- The Hylorans came from Hylor, across the Great Western Ocean.
- They founded the kingdom of Anduir, which spread its influence across Novis and northwestern Jorvarah.
- Over a century ago, Anduir fell in a series of bloody internal wars.
- Hylorans then spread east and south.

## The present

- H'Zhari power is divided among successor states.
- Hyloran communities are a diaspora rather than a single kingdom.
- Sarossians wander widely for adventure and glory.
- Kythari pursue ritualized scholarship and forbidden knowledge.
- Essanin travel and trade in their beetle-shell wagons.
- Draga'Kyr serve as mercenaries.
- Ahzghalid hold mountain cities and keeps.
- Sabbatean tribes live along the western and southern coasts and in the Black Desert, sometimes as mercenaries or bandits.
