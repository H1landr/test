# Ahzghalid

**Ancestry:** Dwarf  
**Range:** Mountains and hills of Northern Jorvarah  
**Chief deity:** [Kharnigh Kharl](../deities.md)

## At a glance

- Also called **Stone dwarves**.
- One of two main dwarven cultures; the other is the secretive [Khazzahlid](khazzahlid.md), or red dwarves.
- Far more numerous than the Khazzahlid.
- Have many holds and cities among the mountains and hills of Northern Jorvarah.
- Reserved with outsiders; only proven friends are invited to their mountain homes.

## Physical traits

- Pale skin bordering on stone grey.
- Eyes slightly larger than human eyes, allowing them to see in even the darkest places.
- Thick beards, carefully groomed and braided.

## Culture and reputation

The Ahzghalid are ancient, tough, and slow to trust. Their mountain holds are centers of dwarven craft and tradition, but access is a privilege rather than a right.

## Source-derived hooks

- A closed hold that must be earned open.
- A craft debt or friendship that grants access.
- Tension between the Ahzghalid and the reclusive Khazzahlid.

## Mechanics

The source says they can see in even the darkest places, but does not define this as infravision or another mechanical trait. Confirm before treating it as a rule.
