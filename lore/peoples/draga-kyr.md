# Draga'Kyr

**Ancestry:** Gargantua / Trollkin  
**Range:** Traveling mercenary bands  
**Chief deity:** Not specified

## At a glance

- Known to most other peoples as **Trollkin** because of their regenerative abilities.
- Origin is unknown: servant race of the Elder Gods, travelers from another plane, or a sorcerer's experiment.
- Fearsome warriors who travel looking for mercenary work.
- A charge of Draga'Kyr has won many battles.
- They have a mysterious affinity with the [Essanin](essanin.md) and are often found with them.

## Physical traits

- Tower over most humans, often by a full head or more.
- Broader and bulkier than humans.
- Hard, leathery grey skin with a pebble-like texture.
- Ram-like horns on the head.
- Long black hair braided into a tail by both males and females.

## Mechanics

The house rules give Draga'Kyr increased healing:

- An 8-hour rest heals `d3` HP.
- A 24-hour rest heals `3d3` HP.

See [the house rules](../../rules/house-rules.md) for the full entry.
