# Essanin

**Ancestry:** Elf  
**Range:** Traveling; distant from the Ilmyrrian  
**Chief deity:** [Leyllwynll](../deities.md)

## At a glance

- A peaceful, gregarious elven people.
- Travel vast distances in great beetle-shell wagons.
- The means of travel are kept secret.
- Master craftsmen and traders.
- Distant relatives of the warlike, empire-building [Ilmyrrian](ilmyrrian.md).

## Physical traits

- Much shorter and slighter than the Ilmyrrian.
- Most have dark hair and large, luminous eyes.
- Arms and shoulders are often covered in swirling tattoos honoring the ancestor god Leyllwynll.

## Culture and reputation

The Essanin are mobile craftspeople and traders, likely welcome in many places but private about their travel methods. Their affinity with the [Draga'Kyr](draga-kyr.md) is noted as mysterious, and the two peoples are often found together.

## Source-derived hooks

- A beetle-shell caravan arriving with goods, news, and secrets.
- A craft object or tattoo with ancestral significance.
- Tension between Essanin peacefulness and Ilmyrrian aggression.
