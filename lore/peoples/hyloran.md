# Hyloran

**Ancestry:** Human  
**Range:** Diaspora from Hylor and the fallen kingdom of Anduir  
**Chief deity:** [Mitra](../deities.md)

## At a glance

- Originate from Hylor, a fabled land across the Great Western Ocean.
- Founded the kingdom of Anduir, which spread its influence across Novis and northwestern Jorvarah.
- Anduir fell over a century ago in a series of bloody internal wars.
- Hylorans have since spread east and south.

## Physical traits

- Tall, fair-haired, and light-eyed.
- Men are often clean-shaven.
- Warriors who follow the old tradition may tattoo the radiant blue sun of Hylor on their foreheads as a mark of loyalty.

## Culture and reputation

Hylorans are described as stoic, laconic, and fearless in battle. Their identity is shaped by a lost kingdom and a sun god, making them a diaspora culture with strong memories and possible rival claims.

## Source-derived hooks

- Refugees, mercenaries, or nobles from the fallen Anduir.
- Old loyalties marked by the blue sun tattoo.
- Tension between stoic reserve and the need to rebuild.
