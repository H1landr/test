# H'Zhari

**Ancestry:** Human  
**Range:** Northern and Eastern Jorvarah; now divided among smaller kingdoms and city-states  
**Chief deity:** [Azura the Eternal Flame](../deities.md)

## At a glance

- The most widespread human culture in the known setting.
- Over a thousand years ago, their cavalry armies overthrew the demon-haunted Empire of Acheron.
- Their unified dominion has since fractured into smaller kingdoms and city-states.
- They value truth as the highest virtue.

## Physical traits

- Olive skin, dark hair, and dark eyes.
- Medium height and build.
- Men wear hair and beards in tight, oiled curls.
- Women wear hair in elaborate braids.

## Culture and reputation

The H'Zhari are described as ancient and sophisticated. Their imperial past and fragmented present make them a natural source of rival successor states, old claims, and contested legitimacy.

## Source-derived hooks

- A successor state may claim authority it no longer holds.
- An old H'Zhari military tradition may survive in a diminished or mercenary form.
- The virtue of truth may create expectations, oaths, or diplomatic friction.
