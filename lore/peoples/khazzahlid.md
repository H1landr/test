# Khazzahlid

**Ancestry:** Dwarf  
**Status:** Mentioned only

## What is known

- Also called **red dwarves**.
- The other main dwarven culture besides the [Ahzghalid](ahzghalid.md).
- Described as secretive.
- No physical description beyond the "red dwarf" name, no homeland, deity, or current situation is given.
