# Kythari

**Ancestry:** Human  
**Range:** The Kythar plateau  
**Chief deity:** [Bahemon](../deities.md)

## At a glance

- Kythar existed before Anduir, before the H'Zhari, and before Acheron.
- The Kythari study the heavens and pursue esoteric secrets.
- Their astrologers are said to rival the elves in knowledge of the stars.
- Their customs are ritualized and byzantine.
- They have an insatiable desire for knowledge, especially forbidden knowledge.

## Physical traits

- Unusually tall and thin.
- Said to look more akin to the elder races than to other humans.
- Tawny skin flecked with gold; orange hair.
- Large almond eyes, often striking blue or grey.

## Culture and reputation

The Kythari are an ancient scholarly people whose love of hidden knowledge makes them natural patrons, rivals, or dangerous experts. Their age and astrological skill may give them access to lore others have lost.

## Source-derived hooks

- A Kythari scholar who wants access to forbidden archives.
- Horoscopes, omens, or celestial cycles that matter to the campaign.
- An old Kythari site or observatory.
