# Sabbatean

**Ancestry:** Half-orc  
**Range:** Western and Southern coasts of Jorvarah; the Black Desert  
**Chief deity:** Not specified

## At a glance

- Once bonded servants of the ancient Acheronean Empire.
- Made up the bulk of Acheron's armies and spread fire and death through Jorvarah.
- Today most tribes travel the hills and mountains of the Western and Southern coasts, eking out a meager existence.
- Others live in the wastes of the [Black Desert](../../places/black-desert.md), surviving by mercenary work and banditry.

## Physical traits

- Broad-shouldered, stocky, and hunched, making them appear somewhat shorter than most humans.
- Deep olive skin with an almost greenish hue.
- Straight, dull, lusterless black hair.
- Ritual scarring on cheeks, chest, and arms.
- Warriors file their teeth to points.

## Culture and reputation

Sabbateans are described as dour, humorless, and single-minded once committed to a task. Their history as Acheronean soldiers shapes how others see them, and their present circumstances range from hardscrabble tribal life to mercenary work and banditry.

## Source-derived hooks

- A Sabbatean mercenary band with a disputed contract.
- Old Acheronean loyalty or equipment surviving in a tribe.
- Conflict between settled communities and desert raiders.
