# Sarossian

**Ancestry:** Human  
**Range:** Sarossian peninsula and the isles of the Middle Sea  
**Chief deity:** [Kleosiros the Red Lion](../deities.md)

## At a glance

- A seafaring and wandering people from the Sarossian peninsula and Middle Sea isles.
- Many travel far for adventure and glory.
- Their pantheon is made up of great heroes who rose to godhood.
- Kleosiros the Red Lion is chief among them.

## Physical traits

- Honey-colored skin.
- Dark hair.
- Eyes of many colors, including a deep purple called **gennet**.
- Those with gennet eyes are said to be blessed by the fates.

## Culture and reputation

Sarossians are described as a people of great moods: roaring laughter, gloomy melancholy, and furious rage. Their heroic tradition makes them natural adventurers, rivals, and legend-carriers.

## Source-derived hooks

- A Sarossian hero-seeker chasing a name or prophecy.
- A gennet-eyed character treated as blessed, cursed, or both.
- Old heroic sagas that may be exaggerated or true.
