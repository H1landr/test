# World Overview

## The known frame

The supplied cultures document names several large regions and peoples:

| Name | What is known | Certainty |
|---|---|---|
| **Jorvarah** | A major region whose northern and eastern lands were once ruled by the H'Zhari; also home to Hyloran influence, Ahzghalid holds, and Sabbatean tribes. | `confirmed` in source |
| **Novis** | A region influenced by the Hyloran kingdom of Anduir. | `confirmed` in source |
| **Hylor** | A fabled land across the Great Western Ocean, ancestral home of the Hylorans. | `reported` / legendary |
| **The Sarossian peninsula** | Homeland of the Sarossians, along with the isles of the Middle Sea. | `confirmed` in source |
| **Kythar** | An ancient plateau civilization, older than Anduir, the H'Zhari, and Acheron. | `confirmed` in source |
| **Acheron** | A demon-haunted empire overthrown by the H'Zhari over a thousand years ago. | `confirmed` in source |
| **The Black Desert** | Desert region west and northwest of Arakay. | `confirmed` on map |
| **The Jashra Mountains** | Mountain range south and southeast of Arakay. | `confirmed` on map |
| **The Middle Sea** | Sea to the north, reached by the river road from Arakay. | `confirmed` on map |

The regional map places **Arakay** between the Black Desert and the Jashra Mountains, at a river and road junction. The map does not label which larger political or geographic region Arakay belongs to.
