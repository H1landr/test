# Barking Dog

**Role:** Dwarven tavern master of The Barking Dogs  
**Status:** Alive

## Summary

The dwarven tavern master of The Barking Dogs, a tavern built in a former fighting pit. The sand and the pit walls remain. He is blunt and not especially fond of his trade.

## What the party knows

- Served the party on their first night looking for work.
