## Summary

Went looking for his daughter after she was taken. He did not go down the well, according to the House of Cheap Delights; the charcoalers were supposed to know where he went.

A charcoaler says he was an occasional customer and was at the top of the stairs a couple of nights ago, "not last week". The two accounts do not agree. The charcoalers are ambivalent to detail rather than deliberately misleading.
