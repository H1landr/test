# Kollaro

**Aliases:** Wakhar  
**Role:** Barber guild member; merchant  
**Status:** Alive  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A merchant who hired the party under the false name Wakhar to escort him to the merchant quarter. The literators ambushed the group to kill him. Kollaro is a member of the Barbers' guild, wanted the captured literator murdered, and paid the party 15 gp each after they refused and handed him over. He named him Mahir at the handover, and said the literators keep their guild in the library.

## What the party knows

- Real name is Kollaro, not Wakhar.
- Barber guild member. The captured leader said he was high in the guild.
- The literators wanted him dead.
- Told the party the literators keep their guild in the library.
- Named the captured literator **Mahir** at the handover.
- Asked the party to murder a bound prisoner; they refused.
- Paid 15 gp each.
- Mentioned a Chül in the city who buys unwanted gear.
