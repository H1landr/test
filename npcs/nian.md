# Nian

**Role:** Rita and Hiram's daughter
**Status:** Missing
**First seen:** Session 2

## Summary

A child of the House of Cheap Delights. She went out on a misty night and did not come back. Her mother drowned herself a week later; her father went looking and has not returned.

The mist is the same mist the urchins blame for the Splinter.

## What the party knows

- Taken on a misty night.
- She knew she was supposed to stay inside.
- She did not like doing as she was told.
- Hiram went looking for her; the charcoalers place him at the top of the stairs a couple of nights ago.
