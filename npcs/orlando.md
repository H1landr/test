# Orlando Danarosa

**Role:** Lady Danarose's missing son, of House Danarosa
**Status:** Missing; the urchins say the Splinter took him, and his mother says he went down after it
**First seen:** Mentioned in Session 1

## Summary

Orlando Danarosa, a boy of House Danarosa, has gone missing in Arakay. The urchins tell the party that he is Lady Danarose's son, and that the Splinter took him. Nobody has seen him since. The rough notes call him "Orlando Bloom"; the clean version shortens it to Orlando, and the DM's Discord list (2026-10-27) gives the surname **Danarosa**.

Session 2 gives him a motive. He wanted to befriend the urchins, and Pablo did not like him. He took his father's star-metal sword to impress Pablo, then went down to fight the Splinter to show off and gain standing in the street. His mother believes the sword being down there was part of his problem.

## What the party knows

- Missing for 48 hours as of Session 2. The urchins are the ones who first said so.
- Wanted to befriend the urchins; Pablo did not like him.
- Took the star-metal sword to impress Pablo.
- Went to fight the Splinter to show off and gain street standing.
- Carried the sword down with him. His mother does not want it back.
- His mother will pay on his return.
