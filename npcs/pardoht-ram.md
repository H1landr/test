# Pardoht-Ram

**Role:** Lady Danarose's steward
**Status:** Alive
**First seen:** Session 2

## Summary

The man who speaks for Lady Danarose. Long white beard, and enough authority to answer the door, set the terms, and invite strangers into the courtyard. The household speaks H'Zhari; he translates for the Lady regardless.

His name was taken down by ear at the table and the archive carried it as Poddat'Ram. The DM's Discord list (2026-10-27) gives **Pardoht-Ram**, which is now the spelling in use. The file name keeps the old spelling so existing links stay valid.

## What the party knows

- Anger at Dio's presence is immediate and personal, which the party reads as urchins being blamed for Orlando.
- States the terms: the chest as reward, 25 gp up front.
- Gives Dagen Orlando's drawing.
- Says no rival group has come looking, only people after a handout.
- Does not know about the guilds; the Lady does not either.
