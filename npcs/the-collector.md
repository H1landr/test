# The Collector

**Role:** Sorcerer said to live in the tunnels
**Status:** Unknown; not met
**First seen:** Session 2

## Summary

A rumour Lady Danarose has heard: a powerful sorcerer living under Arakay who collects "wonders" and information. Nobody in the party has met her, and nothing confirms she exists.

The domed hall of taxidermy below the shanty ruin would suit a collector, but that is the party's guess, not a fact.

## What the party knows

- Described as a powerful sorcerer.
- Said to live in the tunnels.
- Said to collect wonders and information.
- Few of the urchins liked her, according to Dio.
- Mnarka names the Collector a woman, and puts the entrance to her hall below the Barking Dogs tavern. Her sex is canon as of 2026-11-01; the hall entrance is his account, untested.
