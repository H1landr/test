Sheets and what they fix

Only Elowyn's sheet has been supplied, in three versions, the latest dated 2026-11-01. That sheet supplies his stats, his saving throws, his equipment, and his thief skills. It is the reason his experience total carries the +10% prime requisite bonus and the other four do not: their prime requisites have not been declared.

  • Weapon proficiencies are declared for Elowyn only: normal sword, short bow, dagger.
  • The other four sit at the base 752 XP each, with no prime requisite bonus recorded.
  • A sheet that shows a bonus raises that character's total; nothing is assumed before then.

Dio

An urchin of eight from the ruin north-west of the plaza, hired to lead the party to Lady Danarose's house and then kept on as torchbearer. The DM classed him as a hireling rather than an NPC on 2026-10-27.
