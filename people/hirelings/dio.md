# Dio

**Role:** Urchin torchbearer
**Age:** 8
**Status:** Hireling; alive
**Employer:** The party
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A boy of eight from the urchin ruin north-west of the Piazza dei Rospi, and the party's hireling. The DM classed him as a **hireling**, not an NPC, on 2026-10-27. He was hired for 1 sp to lead the party to Lady Danarose's house, then Pablo was paid 5 sp so Dio could go with them into the tunnels.

He is the party's torchbearer. His torch is the first light source the giant lantern moths go for.

## Terms of hire

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Fee | 1 sp to guide the party to Lady Danarose |
| Paid to Pablo | 5 sp for letting Dio go along |
| Tip | 1 ep for silence at the urchin ruin |
| Share of XP or treasure | None recorded; as a hireling he takes no share of exploration XP |

## What the party knows

- Knows the way through the slums to Lady Danarose.
- Was hired, not bought.
- Led the party with the seriousness of a child doing important work.
- Says bad men, meaning guild men, throw people into the well, not urchins.
- Told the party about Rita and Nian before they reached the House of Cheap Delights.
- Says few of the urchins liked the Collector.
- Carried the torch underground and lost it to a moth.
- Was tipped an electrum piece to keep quiet at the urchin ruin.
