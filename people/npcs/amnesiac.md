# The Amnesiac

**Role:** Unknown; a man found in room 7  
**Status:** Dead, killed by Dagen  
**First seen:** Session 3  

## Summary

A man sitting against the wall of room 7 with a candleholder beside him. He remembered nothing and loved counting. Ed tried ventriloquism to talk him out of his stick; it failed, and the man used the stick, which shot a bolt for 2 damage. He won initiative; Bozz and Anglad missed; Dagen opened his throat. On him and around him were eight candles, the candleholder, a leather medallion bearing a candle, and the stick, which is a wand of magic missile.

## What the party knows

- He could not remember anything.
- He liked counting.
- He sat against the wall, alone, with a candleholder beside him.
- His stick is a wand of magic missile: it fires a bolt for 2 damage, its number of charges is unknown, and its command word is "Shras!".
- He carried eight candles and a leather medallion bearing a candle.
- He is dead.

## What is not established

- Who he was, and what took his memory.
- Why he was in room 7, and how long he had been there.
- Whether the wand was his or had been left with him.
- What the leather medallion bearing a candle signifies.
- Whether anyone is looking for him.
