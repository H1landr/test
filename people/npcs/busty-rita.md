# Busty Rita

**Role:** Mother of Nian; worked at the House of Cheap Delights
**Status:** Dead
**First seen:** Session 2

## Summary

Nian's mother. Her daughter went out on a misty night and was taken. A week before Session 2, Rita went to the Well of Frogs and threw herself in.

Dio tells the story at the Danarose door before the party has ever heard her name; the house confirms it later.

## What the party knows

- Her daughter Nian was taken on a misty night.
- Nian knew she was meant to stay inside and did not always do as she was told.
- Rita drowned herself in the well a week ago, after the loss.
- Hiram, Nian's father, went looking for the girl afterward.
