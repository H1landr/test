# Lady Danarose

**Role:** Head of House Danarosa; mother of the missing Orlando Danarosa
**Status:** Alive; met in Session 2
**First seen:** Mentioned in Session 1; met in Session 2

## Summary

The mother of the missing Orlando Danarosa, and the party's employer. She receives them veiled in black mourning dress, with an elaborate headdress of pearls and muted gold medallions, and does not speak to them directly: Pardoht-Ram, her steward, translates for her even though the household speaks H'Zhari.

**Name:** The DM's Discord list (2026-10-27) gives **Lady Danarose** and **House Danarosa**. Pages written before that date used Denarosa for both. The file name keeps the old spelling so existing links stay valid.

She pays 25 gp up front and promises a chest of treasure on Orlando's return.

## What the party knows

- Has a missing son named Orlando Danarosa, gone 48 hours as of Session 2.
- Believes the Splinter took him, and that Orlando went down after it.
- Says Orlando wanted to befriend the urchins, but Pablo did not like him.
- Says Orlando took his father's star-metal sword to impress Pablo, then went to fight the Splinter to show off and gain standing.
- Does not want the sword back, and thinks its being down there was part of Orlando's problem.
- Knows nothing of the guilds, but knows the deep tunnels were built by Acheron.
- Has heard of the Collector: a sorcerer in the tunnels who collects wonders and information.
- Has paid 25 gp up front and promised a chest of treasure on delivery.
- Says no rival group has come, only people looking for a handout.
