# Mahir

**Role:** Literator guild member; led the ambush party  
**Status:** Unknown; handed to the Barbers' guild  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A literator who led the crossbow ambush at the crossroads. Elowyn's first stroke opened one of his men; Bozz took Mahir alive. While bound he gave up his employer's real name, Kollaro of the Barbers' guild, which is what the party needed to hear. Kollaro asked for him to be killed; the party refused and walked him to Barber's Alley instead. Kollaro named him only at the handover.

## What the party knows

- He is a literator, and his name is Mahir.
- He commanded the ambush that was meant to kill Kollaro.
- He talked once captured, and named Kollaro.
- He was beaten and bound, not wounded badly.
- He was delivered alive to the barbers, and Kollaro named him there.
- He had 36 sp, 5 ep, and 8 cp on him, taken by Ed.

## What is not established

- Whether he was born in Arakay or came to the library from elsewhere.
- What the barbers did with him.
- Whether he held real rank in the guild or only led this one job.
- Whether he and Kollaro had met before.
