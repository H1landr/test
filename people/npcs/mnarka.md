# Mnarka

**Role:** Kessian bookkeeper, known as the Archivist  
**Status:** Alive  
**First seen:** Session 3  
**Formerly recorded as:** Manarko, corrected 2026-11-01

## Summary

The man who keeps the book chamber in the undercroft, room 6 on the annotated map. He wears well-kept robes, has dark skin and a neat beard, and his eastern door is marked "knock first" in the trade tongue. He is a Kessian bookkeeper, and is known as the Archivist. He traded the party a spellbook for the two clay tablets, and he will pay in knowledge for the name of whoever was trying to dig into his chamber. The party promised to come back once the children were found.

## What the party knows

- Known as the Archivist; his name is Mnarka.
- A Kessian bookkeeper.
- His chamber is room 6, entered by the eastern door under the "knock first" sign.
- The western door of his chamber leads to the tunnels toward the well of frogs.
- He gave the party a crudely drawn map that omits the dead room and draws the pillar room small.
- He says the entrance to the Collector's hall is below the Barking Dogs tavern, and calls the Collector a woman.
- He traded a spellbook for the two clay tablets. The book holds six arcane spells: Floating Disc, Light, Protection from Evil, Read Languages, Invisibility, Web. Ed is the only member of the party who can use it.
- He calls the origami creatures the paperkin, and says they stay to one shelf.
- He says the well of frogs houses evil, previously worshipped by Acheron.
- He says the skulls came from "those hooked on the coal willing to do dark deeds".
- He does not know where the animals in the domed hall came from, or who the wild man is.

## What is not established

- What a Kessian is, and where his people come from.
- How long he has lived in the undercroft, and how he is provisioned.
- Why someone was trying to dig into his chamber.
- Whether the gaps in his map are carelessness or intent.
- Whether he can leave the chamber, and whether anything guards him.
