# Nook the Older

**Role:** Owner and manager of the Broken Wheel  
**Status:** Alive  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A small, wiry, middle-aged man who owns and manages the Broken Wheel in the northeast corner of the Old Quarter. He wants his money up front. Wafik works there as porter and Rewen as laundress.

## What the party knows

- Runs the Broken Wheel.
- Charges 1 gp per head for lodging.
- Employs Wafik and Rewen.
- Is cautious with strangers and wants payment in advance.
