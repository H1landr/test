# Pablo

**Role:** Oldest of the urchins
**Status:** Alive
**First seen:** Session 1
**Last seen:** Session 2

## Summary

The oldest of the four urchins at the ruin north-west of the Piazza dei Rospi, and the one who does the talking. In Session 1 he arrived after the cakes with Dio at his shoulder, named the Splinter, and told the party that Lady Danarose's son Orlando was missing. He brought Dio forward to be hired and took a silver of his own.

Session 2 supplied his name. He remains the urchins' spokesman: he arranged for the party to take Dio with them, and he is the one who reads the city's politics.

## What the party knows

- About thirteen, with a broken front tooth and a rope burn on one wrist.
- Did the talking for the urchins in Session 1 and named the Splinter first.
- Brought Dio forward to guide the party, and was paid 1 sp for it.
- Says few of the urchins liked the Collector, and that they did not care about Orlando's sword.
- Did not like Orlando. Says Orlando wanted to befriend the urchins and took the star-metal sword to impress him.
- Knows the Literators hold hideouts in the sewers, and that the Barbers are hunting for them.
- Expects a guild war soon, Barbers against Literators, and says the Barbers would pay for a Literator hideout.
- Took 5 sp in Session 2 to let Dio go with the party.
