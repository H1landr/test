# The wild man

**Role:** Unknown  
**Status:** Gone  
**First seen:** Session 3  

## Summary

A dirty, bearded man who came to the altar room while the party rested. Something fluttered and scuttled at the west door and then stopped, and someone tried the south door; when Anglad looked down the corridor he saw the man peering round the corner. The party offered him safety. He did not believe it, and left.

## What the party knows

- Dirty and bearded.
- He came as near as the corner of the corridor while the party rested in the altar room.
- He refused the party's offer of safety and left.
- Mnarka does not know who he is.

## What is not established

- His name, and where he comes from.
- Why he was in the undercroft, and why he ran from the party.
- Whether he is anyone else the party has heard of.
- Whether he is still below the city.
