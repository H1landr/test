## Appearance

<img src="../../assets/characters/elowyn.png" alt="Elowyn" width="420">

Elowyn is a lean, hollow-cheeked male elf with a long face, dark eyes, and pointed ears. His black hair is unkempt and pulled back, with loose strands falling across his face. A pale scar crosses the bridge of his nose. Dark, swirling tattoos cover his right upper arm, and the fingers of both hands are marked with dark lettering. He wears a mud-stained brown leather jerkin, hooded and sleeveless, laced at the throat, and he has the look of someone who has slept rough and does not mind it. In the portrait he holds a thin metal pick or needle, and his smile is small and tired.

The image is the visual reference for Elowyn, supplied by Ian on 2026-10-22. It does not establish new gear beyond the leather he already carries. See the [portrait source note](../../sources/source-elowyn-portrait.md).

### Weapon proficiencies

Thieves are **semi-martial**: 3 proficiencies at 1st level, one more every 4 levels, and **-3 to attack** with anything else. Declared 2026-10-27:

2. Short bow
3. Dagger

Both weapons he carries are covered, so his attack line is unchanged. A dagger is not in his equipment yet. ## Backstory

> Elowyn's father, Keebler, was a legendary adventurer among the Essanin, one of the few of his peaceful people to take up the wandering blade. He survived dragons, ancient traps, and the curses of forgotten Acheronean ruins, only to be mauled by a drunk owlbear looking for a place to vomit.
>
> Elowyn grew up on a turnip farm at the edge of an Essanin waystation where the great beetle-shell wagons paused to trade, and he learned early that hard work is for people without better options and that turnips don't scream when you set them on fire, though the same cannot be said for his cousins. He became the waystation's troublemaker, then graduated to thief, which he insists is just farming with better profit margins and fewer dirt-related regrets.
>
> His swirling shoulder tattoos, meant to honor the ancestor god Leyllwynll, have been augmented over the years with ink he claims is a forgotten blessing and not, as his mother suspects, a receipt for stolen goods. He visits his father's grave sometimes, leaving cheap whiskey and muttering, "At least I know what killed you," then steals the flowers from the next grave over, because sentimentality is expensive and the dead rarely file complaints with the caravan elders.

The text above is lightly normalized for spacing from the character-sheet notes.

## Character hooks

- **Keebler's grave and death:** Elowyn's father survived dragons, traps, and Acheronean curses, but died to a drunk owlbear.
- **Acheronean ruins:** Keebler's old adventures are a natural source of inherited trouble, maps, or enemies.
- **The Essanin waystation:** Elowyn grew up on a turnip farm at a caravan stop; farming remains a practical background before the thief's life.
- **The tattoos:** A claimed forgotten blessing versus his mother's suspicion that the ink is a receipt for stolen goods.
- **Family:** His mother and cousins are mentioned but unnamed.

These are backstory hooks, not yet campaign events.
