# Arakay and the Black Desert

**Type:** Regional map area  
**Status:** Known from the supplied map  
**Map:** [Arakay and the Black Desert](../assets/maps/arakay-and-the-black-desert.png)

## Summary

Arakay sits at a river crossing and road junction between the Middle Sea to the north, the Jashra Mountains to the south, the Black Desert to the west, Khosura to the northeast, and Argentum to the southwest.

| Feature | Position | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Arakay | Center | Fortified settlement on the river and road junction |
| The river | North–south through Arakay | Runs north toward the Middle Sea and south into the Jashra Mountains; the map does not name it |
| The Black Desert | West and northwest | Desert expanse with mountain symbols |
| The Jashra Mountains | South and southeast | Large mountain range |
| Road to the Middle Sea | North | Follows the river toward the sea |
