# Arakay

**Type:** Walled city  
**Region:** River and road junction between the Black Desert, the Jashra Mountains, and the Middle Sea  
**Status:** Known from map; likely play area  
**Map:** [Arakay city map](../assets/maps/arakay-city-map-highlighted.png)

## Summary

Arakay is a walled, roughly oval city split north–south by a broad central corridor. The surrounding map shows a river running through the settlement, and the city is divided into colored districts: the Old Quarter, Temple Quarter, Palace Quarter, and the Trade Way. Extramural buildings crowd the gates, including the district labeled the Slump outside the northwest wall.

## Session 1 developments

- The river is dead and the trade that depended on it has gone; whole quarters are shuttered and the city is sliding into slum.
- Water and bread cost double their usual price.
- The party lodged at [the Broken Wheel](broken-wheel.md), drank at [the Barking Dogs](barking-dogs.md), and was ambushed near the merchant route to the Piazza dei Rospi.
- The old quarter, Barber's Alley, the Piazza dei Rospi, the Well of Frogs, the urchin ruin, and the broken pillar are now active locations.
