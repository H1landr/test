# The Barking Dogs

**Type:** Tavern  
**Location:** Arakay  
**Status:** Visited  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A tavern built in a former fighting pit. The sand and pit walls remain. Barking Dog, a dwarf, is the tavern master. Wakhar hired the party here for the escort job.

## Known facts

- Former fighting pit converted into a tavern.
- Run by Barking Dog, a dwarven tavern master.
- Wakhar offered the party 5 gp each to escort him.
- A place for rumors and carousing.
