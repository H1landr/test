# The Black Desert

**Type:** Desert region  
**Position:** West and northwest of Arakay  
**Status:** Known from map and cultures document  
**Map:** [Arakay and the Black Desert](../assets/maps/arakay-and-the-black-desert.png)

## Summary

A desert expanse west and northwest of Arakay. The cultures document says some Sabbatean tribes live in the wastes of the Black Desert, surviving by mercenary work and banditry.

## Known facts

- The map shows a broad desert region with mountain symbols.
- The Sabbatean culture page places some of its tribes in the Black Desert.
- No settlements, roads, water sources, or ruins are labeled within the desert on the supplied map.

## Unknowns

- Exact borders and size.
- Whether the desert is expanding, cursed, or simply arid.
- Which Sabbatean tribes are present.
- Whether the name is literal, poetic, or both.
