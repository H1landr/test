# The Broken Pillar

**Type:** Street shrine  
**Location:** Arakay, between the well and the Chül's place  
**Status:** Visited  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A broken pillar of white stone, andesite or near it, carved with a kneeling calf wearing a crown of leaves. Offerings surround it, and people were worshipping there when the party passed.

## Known facts

- White stone, possibly andesite or marble.
- Carved with a kneeling calf wearing a leaf crown.
- Surrounded by offerings.
- People were actively worshipping.
