# The Broken Wheel

**Type:** Flophouse / inn  
**Location:** Northeast corner of the Old Quarter, Arakay  
**Status:** Visited; party lodging  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A cheap flophouse in the northeast corner of the Old Quarter. Nook the Older owns and manages it. Wafik is the porter and Rewen the laundress. The party paid 1 gp each for a night's lodging.

## Known facts

- Nook demands payment up front.
- Wafik works as the porter.
- Rewen works as the laundress.
- The party slept here after the first day's investigations.
