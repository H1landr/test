# The Charcoalers' Stairs

**Type:** Street market and stairway
**Location:** North of the Piazza dei Rospi, beside the broad steps
**Status:** Visited
**First seen:** Session 2

## Summary

A landing of black-robed charcoal dealers around a broad flight of steps. They are desert folk from the southern quarter, and they are ambivalent to detail rather than deliberately misleading. Charcoal here is a drug, breathed in for a transcendent experience.

The stairs lead up to the shanty district, and the top of the stairs is where Hiram was last placed.

## Known facts

- Lotus coal is sold at 5 gp, described as half price; Ed haggles it to 3 gp.
- A sick-looking man buys charcoal and runs off.
- Hiram was an occasional customer.
- A charcoaler places Hiram at the top of the stairs a couple of nights ago, "not last week".
- The dealers are not attached to the Ziggurat complex; they are southern-quarter residents.
