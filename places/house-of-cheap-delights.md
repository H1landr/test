# The House of Cheap Delights

**Type:** Brothel
**Location:** North-east of the Piazza dei Rospi
**Status:** Visited
**First seen:** Session 2

## Summary

Where Busty Rita worked, and where the party goes to follow the missing woman. The visit is without incident. The house supplies the names that matter: Rita, her daughter Nian, and Nian's father Hiram. Two of its employees, **Loy** and **Nyeeve**, are named on the DM's Discord list (2026-10-27), though the record does not say whether either of them spoke with the party.

## Known facts

- Rita threw herself into the well a week before Session 2.
- Nian was taken on a misty night.
- Nian knew she was supposed to stay inside and did not like doing as she was told.
- Hiram went looking for Nian and did not go down the well.
- The house points the party at the charcoalers, by the stairs to the east.
- The house's account of Hiram's timing differs from the charcoalers'.
- Loy and Nyeeve are employees; their duties and standing are unrecorded.
