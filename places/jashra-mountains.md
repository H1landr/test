# The Jashra Mountains

**Type:** Mountain range  
**Position:** South and southeast of Arakay  
**Status:** Known from map  
**Map:** [Arakay and the Black Desert](../assets/maps/arakay-and-the-black-desert.png)

## Summary

A large mountain range dominating the southern and southeastern portion of the regional map. The river shown passing through Arakay continues south into the mountains.

## Known facts

- The range forms the southern/southeastern edge of the mapped region.
- The river enters the mountains south of Arakay.
- No passes, holds, ruins, or settlements are labeled in the range.

## Unknowns

- Whether the mountains are inhabited.
- Whether the Ahzghalid have holds there.
- What lies beyond the range.
- The name of the river that enters the mountains.
