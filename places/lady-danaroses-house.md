# Lady Danarose's House

**Type:** Private house and courtyard
**Location:** West of the Piazza dei Rospi
**Status:** Visited in Session 2
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

An estate fallen into disrepair, entered through a small side door beside a closed wooden gate. Inside is a courtyard of traditional Arakayan architecture: false windows, none of them facing the street.

The household keeps two guards and several servants, serves water and flatbread, and speaks H'Zhari. Lady Danarose does not speak to visitors directly; Pardoht-Ram translates for her.

## Known facts

- The gate is wooden and closed. The smaller side door is answered by a servant.
- Dio is not welcome: Pardoht-Ram is angered by his presence alone.
- The party was received in the courtyard, not deeper in the house.
- The chest of treasure is kept here against Orlando's return.
- The map labels it "Danarosa Home", which matches the DM's house name, **House Danarosa**. The lady herself is **Lady Danarose**; the file name keeps the older spelling so links stay valid.
