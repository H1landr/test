# The Middle Sea

**Type:** Sea  
**Position:** North of Arakay, reached by the river road  
**Status:** Known as a destination  
**Map:** [Arakay and the Black Desert](../assets/maps/arakay-and-the-black-desert.png)

## Known facts

- The regional map labels the northern river route **To the Middle Sea**.
- The Sarossian culture is associated with the Sarossian peninsula and the isles of the Middle Sea.

## Unknowns

- Distance from Arakay.
- Which ports or powers control the sea route.
- Whether the river is navigable all the way to the sea.
