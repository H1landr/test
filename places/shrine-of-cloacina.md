# The Shrine of Cloacina

**Type:** Underground shrine
**Location:** North of the Piazza dei Rospi, west of the House of Cheap Delights
**Status:** Visited
**First seen:** Session 2

## Summary

A domed mud-brick building with a stair down to an oval chamber. Dio names it the shrine of **Cloacina**, goddess of sewers. It is lit by candles and incense, holds a statue of the goddess at its centre, and has no attendants.

## Known facts

- Two channels run down the sides of the chamber into a 3 ft drain heading down.
- A central drain is covered by an iron grate and appears to go deep.
- Anglad finds no false walls. He lights a candle before leaving.
- The shrine is marked on the labelled players' map as "Shrine of Sewer Goddess".
