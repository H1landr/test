# The Trade Way

**Type:** District and western approach  
**Position:** Southwest Arakay  
**Status:** Named on the city map  
**Map:** [Arakay city map](../assets/maps/arakay-city-map-highlighted.png)

## Known facts

- A green district occupies the southwestern portion of the walled city.
- The label **The Trade Way** sits near the western approach.
- A road enters the city from the west/southwest.

## Unknowns

- Whether the name refers to the intramural district, the road outside, or both.
- What goods or markets define the area.
- Who controls the gates and traffic.

## Narrative use

The district is a natural place for merchants, caravans, inns, and gate traffic, but those details are not confirmed by the map.
