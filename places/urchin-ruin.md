# The Urchin Ruin

**Type:** Ruined building / urchin gathering place  
**Location:** North-west of the Piazza dei Rospi, Arakay  
**Status:** Visited  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

A ruin north-west of the plaza where four urchins gather. The party brought sesame cakes and traded captured weapons for information. Dio was hired here as a guide.

## Known facts

- Four urchins in all. Two came out for the cakes, then the oldest, **Pablo**, arrived with Dio.
- Pablo is the oldest and does the talking for the pack.
- Cakes buy more than silver with the children.
- The urchins know the well is unsafe.
- They call the well monster the Splinter.
- They identified Orlando as Lady Danarose's missing son.
- Dio agreed to guide the party for 1 sp.
