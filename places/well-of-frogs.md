# The Well of Frogs

**Type:** Ancient well  
**Location:** Piazza dei Rospi, Arakay  
**Status:** Visited; cursed reputation  
**First seen:** Session 1

## Summary

An old stone well ringed by four statues of horned bull frogs, each about the size of a dog. Locals say the well is older than Arakay and cursed. The shaft is lined with frog carvings and seems bottomless by lantern light. The urchins call the thing below **the Splinter**.

## Known facts

- Four horned bull-frog statues surround the mouth.
- The well is said to be older than the city.
- People avoid drawing water from it.
- Frog carvings line the shaft.
- Ed lowered a lantern and saw no bottom.
- One carving appeared to turn and croak.
- The urchins say the Splinter takes children down the well.
- The water below is green.

## Rumors

- Urchins sometimes throw one of their own into the well.
- A humanoid monster stalks the neighborhood on misty nights.
- A frog monster drags down anyone who ventures too close.
- The last person to draw water came from the House of Cheap Delights.
