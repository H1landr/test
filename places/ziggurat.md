# The Ziggurat

**Type:** Large civic or religious complex  
**Position:** Southern Arakay, adjacent to the Palace Quarter  
**Status:** Named on the city map  
**Map:** [Arakay city map](../assets/maps/arakay-city-map-highlighted.png)

## Known facts

- The city map labels a large black rectangular complex **Ziggurat**.
- It sits at the southern edge of the walled city, next to the Palace Quarter.
- The complex appears to have its own boundary or precinct.

## Inferred, not confirmed

- The name suggests a stepped temple or monumental structure, but the map shows a rectangular footprint rather than a stepped plan.
- Its proximity to the Palace Quarter may indicate a state or royal cult, but this is not established.

## Unknowns

- Deity, cult, or function.
- Whether it is open to the public.
- Who guards or controls it.
- Whether the black fill indicates shadow, stone, or map emphasis.

## Narrative use

The Ziggurat is the strongest visual landmark in southern Arakay. Use it as a silhouette and orientation point; do not assign its cult or purpose until play establishes it.
