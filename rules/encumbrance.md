# Item-Based Encumbrance

Reference for [HR-03](house-rules.md). Rules by Gavin Norman, transcribed from the supplied *Carcass Crawler #2* pages.

## Core procedure

Track the number of **significant items** carried instead of coin weight.

- An object carried in one hand counts as **1 item**.
- An object requiring two hands counts as **2 items**.
- Very heavy or bulky objects may count as **3 or more items**, by referee judgment.
- Each item is either **equipped** or **packed**.
- Look up both totals on the movement table and use the **slower** result.

### Equipped items

Anything held, actively used, or ready at short notice:

- Armour worn.
- Shields or weapons held.
- Sheathed weapons.
- Items worn on the belt.

### Packed items

Everything else, packed into sacks, backpacks, and similar containers. Retrieving a packed item in combat optionally takes one round.

## Movement rate

| Equipped items | Packed items | Movement rate |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | 0–10 | 120' (40') |
| 4–5 | 11–12 | 90' (30') |
| 6–7 | 13–14 | 60' (20') |
| 8–9 | 15–16 | 30' (10') |

**Maximum load:** a character carrying more than 9 equipped items or more than 16 packed items cannot move.

## Weapons and armour

- **One-handed weapon:** 1 item.
- **Two-handed weapon:** 2 items.
- **Ammunition:** the encumbrance of a missile weapon already includes its ammunition and container.
- **Shield:** 1 item.
- **Light armour** (e.g. leather): 1 item.
- **Heavy armour** (e.g. chainmail): 2 items.
- **Clothing** (i.e. unarmoured): does not count as encumbering.

### Bundles

Some items are usually bought and carried in bundles. Up to **3 individual items** bundled together count as **1 item**. The table lists the item weight of the number of individual items in the bundle when bought; for example, 7 rations count as 3 items.

**Table convention:** on the Well of Frogs character sheet, each filled line in the Equipped or Packed section is one slot. Do not break a bundle or the backpack into extra slots for the sheet's movement calculation. Elowyn's sheet therefore reads 4 equipped / 12 packed.

### Tiny items

Very small items, such as necklaces or rings, do not count as encumbering unless carried in large numbers.

### Storage

Containers such as backpacks and sacks count as an item only when **not in use**. The referee judges how much can be stored in a container.

## Treasure

- **Coins and gems:** up to 100 coins or gems count as **1 item**.
- **Jewellery:** tiny pieces do not count; larger pieces count as **1 item**.
- **Magic items:** staves count as **2 items**; other magic items count as **1 item**.

## Optional STR modifier

A character's melee STR modifier may be applied to the number of packed items allowed at each movement rate band.

Example: a character with STR 16 (`+2`) can carry up to 12 packed items at 120' (40').

## Example

Morgan, a fighter, carries:

- **6 equipped items:** chainmail (2), shield (1), sword (1), shortbow (2, including quiver and arrows).
- **4 packed items:** rope (1), tinderbox (1), 3 iron rations (bundle, 1), waterskin (1).

Morgan's equipped total gives 60' (20'); his packed total gives 120' (40'). He uses the slower rate: **60' (20')**.
