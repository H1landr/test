# Feats of Exploration

An award system layered on top of OSE's monster and treasure XP. It pays experience for exploring campaign environments: finding secrets, confirming rumours, overcoming hazards, using gear cleverly, and the like.

**Source:** [Feats of Exploration](../sources/source-feats-of-exploration.md)  
**Authority:** The PDF is authoritative. Ian's referee runs it at the table. This page is a working reference.

## The three XP streams

| Stream | Notes |
|---|---|
| Monsters defeated | Standard OSE |
| Treasure attained | Standard OSE |
| Feats of Exploration | This system; see below |

## Award values

Each feat is worth a percentage of **TXP**, not a flat number.

| Tier | Share of TXP | Typical use |
|---|---:|---|
| Minor | 2% | Normal exploration challenges; the common case |
| Major | 5% | Less common, or a harder version of a minor feat |
| Extraordinary | 10% | Major campaign developments |
| Campaign | 15% | Bespoke, extremely difficult, whole-campaign goals |

## TXP and party TXP

- **TXP** is the XP a PC needs to advance from the start of their current level to the next. A 1st-level thief's TXP is 1,200, because 1,200 is the requirement for 2nd level.
- **Party TXP** is the sum of TXP for every PC present. Recalculate it when the party make-up changes or a PC gains a level.
- **Retainers are excluded.** They get no Exploration XP and are not counted when dividing it. They still receive monster and treasure XP under the normal rules.

## Feats

| Feat | Definition |
|---|---|
| Exploration | Explore at least 5 areas of a single dungeon level |
| Lore | Apply in-world lore in a useful or flavourful manner |
| Rumor | Confirm a rumour's veracity |
| Secret | Find a secret or interpret hidden lore |
| Puzzle | Solve a puzzle |
| Trap | Overcome a trap |
| Hazards | Surmount an environmental obstacle or hazard |
| Skills | Use equipment or abilities in an unorthodox but useful manner |
| Location | Discover an important location |
| NPC | Interact beneficially with an important NPC when stakes are at play |
| Faction | Manipulate or cripple a faction to your benefit |
| Quest | Complete a quest |
| Safe Haven | Establish a reliable safe haven |

Feats with both Major and Minor tiers are defined by the referee. There is no cap on instances in a session. Tick a box per instance and note the circumstances.

## Procedure

1. Note who is playing and each PC's level.
2. Calculate TXP per PC and the party TXP.
3. Work out the percentage values: Minor 2%, Major 5%, Extraordinary 10%, Campaign 15% of party TXP.
4. Tick feats during play. A feat counts for the whole party, not only the PC who achieved it.
5. At the end of the session, multiply ticks by the percentage value, total everything, and divide by the number of players present.

## Worked example — Session 2, 2026-10-25

The referee's sheet for Session 2:

| Step | Value |
|---|---|
| Party TXP (implied by the 2% figure) | 9,950 |
| Minor feat value (2%) | 199 |
| Minor feats ticked | Exploration 1, Rumor 2, Skills 1, Trap 1 = 5 instances |
| Feats XP | 5 × 199 = 995 |
| Combat XP | 3 giant lantern moths × 10 = 30 |
| Treasure XP | 25 gp advance + 5 amber gems at 30 gp = 175 |
| Party total | 1,200 |
| Divided five ways | 240 per PC |
| Elowyn, with +10% prime requisite | 264 |

- The 199 figure is confirmed by the sheet's own "XP Value" box, and 199 × 5 = 995, which matches its "Total XP Earned".
- 9,950 is consistent with five first-level PCs at TXP 1,200 (thief), 2,000 (fighter), 2,000 (fighter), 2,250 (ranger), 2,500 (magic user). The ranger figure is inferred from the total, not read off a table. `[?]`
