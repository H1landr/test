## HR-01 — Shields Shall Be Splintered

A character currently using a shield may destroy it to block all damage from an attack.

- **Martial classes** (fighter, barbarian, ranger, etc.) may declare the block **after** the attacker rolls damage.
- **Semi-martial classes** with access to shields may declare the block **before** damage is rolled.
- In either case, the shield is completely destroyed and unusable afterward.
- Normal shields can block only damage from a physical attack.
- Damage from traps, the environment, or poison cannot be blocked by a normal shield.
- Certain magical shields can block magical attacks.

## HR-02 — Always Wear Your Helmet

- Classes that can equip shields can also equip helmets.
- A helmet does **not** provide an AC bonus.
- A helmet can absorb the damage from a single critical hit, then is rendered useless.
- Wearing a helmet imposes a `-1` penalty to Surprise.

## HR-03 — Moving and Encumbrance (Item-Based)

Use the **item-based encumbrance** system from *Carcass Crawler #2* (Gavin Norman, pp. 28–29).

- Track significant items rather than coin weight.
- Classify each item as equipped or packed.
- Look up both totals and use the slower movement rate.
- Full procedure, movement table, and gear weights: [Item-Based Encumbrance](encumbrance.md).

**Source:** [Item-Based Encumbrance](../sources/source-item-based-encumbrance.md)

## HR-04 — Expanded Equipment

The source points to *Carcass Crawler #3* for expanded equipment and item-based encumbrance. Exact scope should be confirmed against the supplement.

## HR-05 — Crossbows and Longbows

| Weapon | Damage | Range | Rules |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Crossbow | `2d4` | 5'–70' / 71'–140' / 141'–210' | Two-handed, Slow, Reload |
| Long bow | `1d6` | 5'–80' / 81'–160' / 161'–240' | Two-handed |

\* If loaded ahead of combat, a crossbow is **not** Slow in the first round.

## HR-06 — Fighter: Favored Weapon Type

At 1st level, a fighter may choose a weapon type preferred over all others. The fighter receives `+1` to hit and `+1` to damage with that weapon type in combat.

## HR-07 — Cleric: Sanctified Weapons

At character creation, a cleric chooses three weapon types as the weapons of their god. These weapons may be used without penalty. Other weapons may not be used. The choices should make sense for the deity.

## HR-08 — Magic-User: Starting Spells

A newly created magic-user begins with **four random Level 1 spells** in their spellbook, in addition to **Read Magic**.

## HR-09 — Thief: D6 Skills

Use the **D6 thief skills** from *Carcass Crawler #1*. The full article is archived at [D6 Thief Skills](../sources/source-thief-d6-skills.md), and the working reference is [Thief Skills (D6)](thief-skills.md).

- All skills start at **1-in-6**.
- A 1st-level thief allocates **4 expertise points**; each point raises one skill by 1-in-6.
- **+2 points per level** thereafter, maximum **5-in-6** per skill.
- **Read languages** is available from 1st level and can be improved, per the article. Note that the same article's later section still repeats the standard "from 4th level" wording; this campaign follows the 1st-level version.

## HR-10 — Ranger: Monster Slayer

A ranger receives `+1` to attack and damage rolls against giants and monstrous creatures larger than a man.

## HR-11 — Detect Magic and Read Magic

### Detect Magic

All magic-users can detect magical resonance at will if they can concentrate without distraction and physically touch the subject.

- Only the **existence** of magic is detected.
- The nature and strength of the magic are not revealed.
- Each attempt requires one turn.

### Read Magic

- Read Magic is **not** necessary to decipher scrolls.
- Read Magic **is** necessary to decipher spellbooks.

## HR-12 — Trollkin: Increased Healing

A Draga'Kyr / Trollkin heals faster than other ancestries:

- An 8-hour rest heals `d3` HP.
- A 24-hour rest heals `3d3` HP.

## HR-13 — Thief Hit Die and Starting HP

This is an additional table clarification, not part of the v1.0 PDF.

- **Thief hit die:** `d6` (2026 OSE DM option).
- **Starting HP:** maximum hit die result plus the CON modifier.
- **Example:** Elowyn, Thief 1, CON 8 (`-1`): `6 - 1 = 5` HP.

The maximum-HP-at-1st-level rule applies to the initial character total. Confirm whether it also applies to newly recruited retainers or replacement characters.

## HR-14 — Weapon Proficiencies

This is an optional OSE rule, adopted on the DM's instruction on 2026-10-27. Proficiencies are declared at 1st level.

- **Martial:** 4 proficiencies at 1st, then one every 3 levels. Non-proficient penalty `-2`.
- **Semi-martial:** 3 at 1st, then one every 4 levels. Non-proficient penalty `-3`.
- **Non-martial:** 1 at 1st, then one every 5 levels. Non-proficient penalty `-5`.
- Weapon specialisation (`+1` attack and damage with one weapon) is open to martial characters only.

Elowyn, a semi-martial thief, is proficient with **normal sword**, **short bow**, and **dagger**. Full reference, including the party's aptitudes: [Weapon Proficiencies](weapon-proficiencies.md).

## HR-15: Splash and Area Attack Deviation

Added at the table on 2026-11-01. When a splash or area attack misses, it deviates from the target.

- **Distance:** it deviates 1 ft for every point by which the attack roll missed.
- **Direction:** roll a `d12`.

| d12 | Where it lands |
|---|---|
| 1 | Bounces back, towards the thrower |
| 2 | Falls short, somewhere in between |
| 3-6 | Goes wide; roll a second `d6`, 1-3 left, 4-6 right |
| 7-12 | Too far, past the target |

A wide throw takes a second roll: `d6`, where 1-3 puts it to the left and 4-6 to the right.
