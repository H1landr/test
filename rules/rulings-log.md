## How to log a ruling

1. State the question in plain language.
2. Record the ruling exactly as it was applied.
3. Note what it affected: HP, XP, loot, time, distance, surprise, morale, or another resource.
4. Mark whether it was a one-time call or a standing interpretation.
5. If it is later changed, keep the old entry and mark it `overturned`.
