# Thief Skills (D6)

The campaign's thief class uses d6 skills rather than OSE's percentage table. This is house rule [HR-09](house-rules.md); the full article is archived at [D6 Thief Skills](../sources/source-thief-d6-skills.md).

## The skills

| Skill | Base | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| Climb sheer surfaces (CS) | 1-in-6 | Failure means a fall partway up |
| Find or remove treasure traps (TR) | 1-in-6 | Treasure traps only; room traps are found by searching |
| Hear noise (HN) | 1-in-6 | Listening at doors, and general noise |
| Hide in shadows (HS) | 1-in-6 | Needs shadow or cover |
| Move silently (MS) | 1-in-6 | Failure still means quiet movement; normal surprise then applies |
| Open locks (OL) | 1-in-6 | Thieves only |
| Pick pockets (PP) | 1-in-6 | Reduced 1-in-6 per three levels of the victim; a 6 is noticed |
| Read languages (RL) | 1-in-6 | From 1st level in this campaign, and improvable |

## Expertise

- All skills begin at **1-in-6**.
- A 1st-level thief has **4 expertise points** to allocate at creation.
- Each point raises one skill by 1-in-6. A skill may take several points.
- **+2 points per level** after 1st.
- **No skill above 5-in-6.**

## At the table

| Situation | Ruling |
|---|---|
| Back-stab | Applies against an unaware opponent; the attack bonus and damage multiplier are as the class ability |
| Climbing | One check per climb; failure risks a fall |
| Hiding | Requires shadow or cover; the thief must be still |
| Sneaking | A failed move silently roll still leaves the thief moving quietly, so normal surprise applies |
| Silent environments | Non-thieves *cannot* sneak unnoticed; thieves roll |
| Noisy environments | Background noise means the normal surprise roll covers the party's approach |
| Locks | Only thieves may attempt |
| Pockets | Only thieves may attempt |
| Repeating a check | Optional: one turn per further attempt, with the usual wandering-monster risk |
| Trap at a door | Elowyn listens and searches; the roll is on d6 |

## Current party thief

**Elowyn**, Devil-may-care, level 1. Values on his sheet: CS 1, TR 2, HN 2, HS 2, MS 2, OL 2, PP 1, RL 1. All accounted for: **HS 2 is the 1-in-6 base plus a +1 elf bonus**, and the four expertise points sit on TR, HN, MS and OL.

## Ancestry bonus

- **Elf:** +1 to hide in shadows, applied on top of the base chance and before expertise. Per Ian, 2026-10-26. The 5-in-6 cap still applies to the final value.

## Worked example — the moth door, Session 2

- No surprise roll was made, because the door was **kicked in**, which removes any chance of surprise. The two kicks also gave the moths time to stir.
- Elowyn's move silently was never tested: no one tried to pass quietly, so the sneaking rules did not come up.
