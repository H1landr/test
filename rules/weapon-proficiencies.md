# Weapon Proficiencies

**Status:** In force. The DM confirmed on 2026-10-27 that the optional weapon proficiency rule is used, and that proficiencies are declared at 1st level.

**Source:** Supplied as a page image in chat, 2026-10-27 (p. 25). The image names the rule "Weapon Proficiency (Optional Rule)" but does not name the book. The image itself is not archived.

## The rule

Without this rule, any weapon a class may use is used at no penalty. With it, each character is **proficient** with a limited number of weapon types, chosen from those their class and race may use. Proficient weapons are used without penalty. Anything else takes a penalty to attack.

| Combat aptitude | Proficiencies at 1st | New proficiency | Non-proficient penalty |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Non-martial | 1 | every 5 levels | **-5** |
| Semi-martial | 3 | every 4 levels | **-3** |
| Martial | 4 | every 3 levels | **-2** |

The book's worked example gives a martial character new proficiencies at **4th, 7th, 10th**, which sets the pattern: the interval counts from 1st level.

So:

- **Martial:** 4 at 1st, then 4th, 7th, 10th, 13th.
- **Semi-martial:** 3 at 1st, then 5th, 9th, 13th.
- **Non-martial:** 1 at 1st, then 6th, 11th, 16th.

## Elowyn's proficiencies

Declared by Ian, 2026-10-27:

1. **Normal sword** (the `1d8` sword on his sheet)
2. **Short bow**
3. **Dagger**

He currently carries the sword and the short bow. The dagger is a proficiency only; no dagger is listed in his equipment yet.

**Effects at the table:**

- No change to his current attacks. Both weapons he carries are proficient.
- Anything else he picks up, including a bow of a different type or a weapon taken from a fallen enemy, is at **-3 to attack**.
- The penalty applies to **back-stab** as well, since that is still an attack roll with a weapon. It does not touch damage, AC, or thief skills.
- His DEX `+2` missile bonus applies on top of the penalty: a non-proficient bow would be `+2 -3 = -1`.
- **No weapon specialisation.** The rule restricts it to martial characters, so the `+1` attack and damage option is closed to him.
- Next proficiency at **5th level**.

If he takes a dagger into the field, check the slot cost first: he is already encumbered at 4 equipped and 12 packed.

## Separate from allowed weapons

The proficiency rule limits training, not legality. Elowyn's sheet says a thief may use all weapons, so he may carry anything; only three weapon types are penalty-free. The class weapon list still governs what a class is permitted to use at all.
