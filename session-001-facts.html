1. The caravan road and the city gate

  • The caravan was destroyed by Zuiagirites in the dunes west of Arakay. The merchants were killed, the wagons burned, the animals stripped.
  • Five survivors walked in: Elowyn, Anglad, Bozz, Dagen, Ed. They carried what they could.
  • Arakay's gate stood open and had not been shut in years.
  • The River Asser is dry. Its bed lies under a bridge that crosses nothing.
  • Water and bread cost double.
  • In the Piazza dei Rospi, the Well of Frogs is ringed by four statues of horned bull frogs the size of dogs. People cross the square without going near the well, and without admitting the detour.
  • Local saying: the well is older than the city, and cursed.

2. The Broken Wheel, then the Barking Dogs

  • The party lodged at the Broken Wheel, north-east corner of the old quarter.
  • Nook the Older: small, wiry, middle-aged; wants payment up front.
  • Wafik: porter.
  • Rewen: laundress under Nook's roof.
  • The next night they went looking for work at the Barking Dogs, a former fighting pit with sand still in the floor and the pit walls standing.
  • Barking Dog: dwarf, keeps the tavern, blunt about his trade.
  • Wakhar hired the party to escort him to the merchant quarter south-west of the Piazza dei Rospi. Five gold per head. They accepted.
  • Wakhar had goods and no iron. Clean boots, soft hands, no knife at his belt.

3. The crossroads ambush

  • The quarter is stepped streets with leaning houses.
  • Terrain: an alley east; a street west with an awning and a stack of rolled carpets; a steep stair behind; an oxcart set square across the way.
  • Dagen stopped and warned the party before the crossbowmen showed themselves. The party acted first.
  • Crossbowmen were at the alley mouth and the street corner.
  • Elowyn reached the nearest one before he could level his weapon and killed him with one stroke, shoulder to hip. The man got no shot off.
  • A bolt rang off Anglad's mail. A bolt stuck in Bozz's shield. A third passed close to Wakhar and hit a shutter.
  • Anglad killed the second crossbowman. The rest fled.
  • The literator ran. Elowyn caught him and struck him with the flat of his sword to put him down. Bozz tackled him, lifted him off his feet, put him on his back and held a point to his jaw.
  • No PC was wounded. The literator was taken alive.

4. Kollaro, Barber's Alley, and the fee

  • The prisoner was a literator, the man who led the ambush party.
  • Ed searched him: 36 sp, 5 ep, 8 cp.
  • Under questioning the literator gave up the name of the man who had hired the party: Kollaro, of the Barbers' guild. The party had known him as Wakhar; Wakhar had been a false name. The crossbows had been meant for Kollaro, not for the goods. The escort was an inconvenience.
  • Kollaro said the literators keep their guild in the library. He said it as though the party already knew. No reason for the feud between the guilds was given or asked for.
  • Kollaro asked the party to kill the prisoner.
  • Elowyn refused: the man was bound and beaten, and killing him would be murder.
  • Kollaro accepted the refusal without argument. He said the literator was no danger now.
  • The party took the literator to Barber's Alley and handed him over to the barbers, who are Kollaro's guild.
  • Kollaro named the prisoner at the handover: Mahir. That was the first the party heard the name.
  • Kollaro paid them 15 gp each: the five promised plus ten more.
  • Kollaro mentioned a Chül in the city who buys unwanted gear.

5. Back to the Barking Dogs

  • The party returned to the Barking Dogs the same day and let the local drinkers talk.
  • They heard three stories about the plaza, all secondhand:
    1. The urchins sometimes toss one of their own down the well.
    2. On mist-shrouded nights, a thing in the shape of a man walks the neighbourhood.
    3. A frog monster lurks in the water and drags under whoever leans too near. The last soul to draw water there came from the House of Cheap Delights.
  • They were also told, as directions, that the urchins gather in a ruin north-west of the plaza.
  • Nobody who told the stories claimed to have seen any of it.
  • The party decided to look at the well.

6. The Well of Frogs

  • Late afternoon in the Piazza dei Rospi.
  • Ed lowered a lantern on a rope into the well.
  • The shaft is lined with rows of frog carvings and appears to have no bottom.
  • One carving moved: a head lifted quickly, a face turned toward the light, the mouth opened. It croaked.
  • The sound carried across the square. A door closed somewhere. Nobody else in the square came near.
  • Ed said aloud what he had seen; the others had heard the croak.
  • He pulled the lantern up. Nothing followed. Nobody descended.

7. The palm reading

  • At the same time as Ed was at the well, an old woman came up behind Dagen and the others.
  • She was old but not yet a crone. Two cats followed her.
  • She offered a reading and asked nothing for it.
  • Dagen gave her his hand.
  • Her reading: they will go down into caverns; there is death there and great reward; keep clear of the green water.
  • The croak from the well carried across the square while she was speaking. She did not startle.
  • She finished, let go of his hand and left. Nobody saw her look back. No name or affiliation was given.

8. Night at the Broken Wheel

  • The party paid 1 gp each for lodging, having received 15 gp each from Kollaro.
  • Wafik asked whether they were joining the Khosuran armies. He said there is a war with the Easterners and the Khosurans are winning, and a man could do well by choosing early.
  • Rewen explained how she and Wafik came under Nook's roof.
  • Dagen kept the prophecy to himself.

9. The pillar and the Chül

  • The party went to a broken white pillar, andesite or near it, carved with a kneeling calf wearing a crown of leaves.
  • Offerings lay at its foot: bread, salt, a knot of coloured thread, a knife with the blade snapped off.
  • Worshippers were present and did not invite questions. No doctrine was learned.
  • Elowyn bought sesame cakes with his own gold. The cakes helped them find the Chül.
  • Batros the Chül trades from a blanket spread on the street, not from a shop.
  • He offered a very low price for the party's two swords and two crossbows. The party kept them.
  • Ed bought a Fire Rock from a pile of them for 5 gp. Thrown hard against stone, it will be heard across the quarter. It has not been tested.
  • Also on the blanket:
    • A green rod of metal that gives off its own light, continuously, in daylight. Batros says chips of it will sharpen a spell. Not bought. Price not recorded.
    • A silvery fabric pouch. It looked expensive once his prices on the other goods had been heard, and nobody asked what it held or what it cost.
    • A necklace that has the look of lapis lazuli, with a cat charm on it, priced at 150 gp. Not bought. Nobody touched it.

10. The urchin ruin

  • The ruin north-west of the plaza held four urchins.
  • Two came out for the cakes. The oldest, Pablo, about thirteen, with a broken front tooth and a rope burn on one wrist, arrived afterward with Dio. Pablo did the talking.
  • The party brought cakes. The urchins took the two swords and two crossbows in exchange for information.
  • What the urchins gave:
    • The water is not safe.
    • The frog monster is real.
    • They call it the Splinter. It takes children and takes them down the well.
    • Lady Danarose's son Orlando is missing.
  • Elowyn argued that they should get an advance from Lady Danarose before going down.
  • The party hired Dio, an urchin boy, for 1 sp to lead them to Lady Danarose's house.
  • Pablo, the oldest boy, brought Dio forward and was given a silver of his own.
  • The session ended on the walk to her house. The party did not arrive, knock, or meet her.

Decisions made by the party

  • Took Wakhar's escort job at 5 gp per head.
  • Fought the ambush rather than fled.
  • Refused Kollaro's request to kill the prisoner.
  • Handed Mahir to the barbers alive.
  • Went back to the Barking Dogs to gather talk.
  • Looked into the well rather than descending.
  • Kept the swords and crossbows when the Chül's offer was low, then traded them to the urchins for information.
  • Bought only one Fire Rock from the Chül, out of a pile of them.
  • Never asked about the silvery fabric pouch on the blanket.
  • Decided to seek an advance from Lady Danarose before entering the well.
  • Hired Dio to guide them to her house.
