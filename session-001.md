# Session 1 — Down and Out in Arakay

**Rough notes:** [Session 1 rough notes](session-001-notes.md)  
**Clean source:** [Down and Out in Arakay](session-001-clean-notes.md)  
**One-line summary:** Five caravan survivors take a bodyguard job in dying Arakay, foil a literator ambush, and learn of a cursed well and a missing child.  
**Narrative outline:** [Session 1 outline](session-001-outline.md)  
**Source confidence:** `high` for events. The literator's name was supplied by your notes on 2026-10-22 and confirmed as coming from Kollaro at the handover.  
**Note-taker:** Max, playing Bozz.  
**Table:** Five PCs; Keith refereeing; no battlemaps.

## Summary

- The party were the survivors of a caravan ambushed on the road; they arrived in Arakay with little but their gear.
- At the Broken Wheel and the Barking Dogs they met Nook the Older, Barking Dog, and the merchant Wakhar.
- Wakhar hired them for 5 gp each to escort him to the merchant quarter southwest of the Piazza dei Rospi.
- At a crossroads, literators ambushed the group with crossbows. Elowyn drew first blood; Bozz captured their leader; the rest fled.
- The literator revealed that Wakhar was really **Kollaro**, a member of the Barbers' guild, and that the literators had come to kill him. Kollaro also said the literators keep their guild in the library. Kollaro wanted the literator murdered; the party refused.
- Kollaro raised the fee and paid them 15 gp each. At the handover he named the prisoner: **Mahir**. He also mentioned a **Chül** in the city who would buy unwanted gear.
- Back at the Barking Dogs after the handover, the party heard three stories about the plaza: urchins sometimes throw one of their own down the well, a man-shaped thing walks the streets on misty nights, and a frog monster drags down anyone who leans too near.
- At the Piazza dei Rospi, Ed lowered a lantern into the Well of Frogs and saw frog carvings in the shaft; one seemed to turn and croak. While he was at the well, an old woman read Dagen's palm.
- The party found the Chül, traded captured weapons to urchins for information, and learned that **the Splinter** takes children down the well. Among the Chül's wares were a green rod of glowing metal, a silvery fabric pouch that looked costly, and a lapis-look necklace with a cat charm priced at 150 gp; the only purchase was one Fire Rock out of the pile, and nobody asked about the pouch.
- Lady Danarose's son **Orlando Danarosa** is missing, as the urchins told them. Elowyn argued for an advance before any rescue attempt; the party hired the boy **Dio** for 1 sp to lead them to her.

## Scene chronology

### Scene 1 — The road and the city

**Location:** The road into Arakay  
**Present:** Elowyn, Anglad, Bozz, Dagen, Ed  
**What happened:** Raiders destroyed the caravan. Five survivors reached Arakay on foot. The city is a diminished trade town: the river died, trade followed, whole quarters are shuttered, and water and bread cost double.  
**Decision:** The party looked for lodging and work.  
**Consequence:** They took rooms at the Broken Wheel in the northeast corner of the Old Quarter.

### Scene 2 — The Broken Wheel and the Barking Dogs

**Location:** Broken Wheel; Barking Dogs tavern  
**Present:** The party; Nook the Older; Barking Dog; Wakhar  
**What happened:** Nook demanded payment up front. At the Barking Dogs, a former fighting pit, Barking Dog, the dwarven tavern master, served them. Wakhar hired the party to escort him to the merchant quarter southwest of the Piazza dei Rospi for 5 gp each.  
**Decision:** They accepted.  
**Consequence:** The job was a trap aimed at Wakhar, not at them.

### Scene 3 — The crossroads ambush

**Location:** A mean quarter of stepped streets and leaning houses; a crossroads  
**Present:** The party; Wakhar; crossbowmen; their leader  
**What happened:** East was an alley; west a street with an awning and rolled carpets; behind a steep stair; an oxcart blocked the center. Crossbowmen emerged. The first bolts went at the merchant. Elowyn charged low and killed one; the party cut down a second; the rest fled. The literator broke and ran. Elowyn chased him, struck him with the flat of his sword to subdue him, and Bozz tackled him immediately after.  
**Decision:** Fight through the ambush rather than flee.  
**Consequence:** The literator was captured; the party learned the ambush was aimed at their employer.

### Scene 4 — Kollaro's truth

**Location:** The crossroads; Barber's Alley  
**Present:** The party; the captive literator leader; Kollaro  
**What happened:** He was a literator. Wakhar's real name was **Kollaro**, a member of the Barbers' guild. The literators had come to kill Kollaro, and Kollaro said in passing that they keep their guild in the library. Ed searched the literator and found 36 sp, 5 electrum, and 8 cp. Kollaro asked the party to kill the prisoner; they refused. They delivered the literator to the barbers, and it was Kollaro who named him there: **Mahir**.  
**Decision:** Refuse murder; hand the literator over alive.  
**Consequence:** Kollaro paid them 15 gp each, but the party learned they had helped him eliminate a rival. They no longer trusted him.

### Scene 5 — Back to the Barking Dogs

**Location:** The Barking Dogs tavern  
**Present:** The party; Barking Dog; local drinkers  
**What happened:** After the handover the party went back to the Barking Dogs and let the talk come to them. They heard three things about the plaza: the urchins sometimes toss one of their own down the well; on mist-shrouded nights a thing in the shape of a man stalks the neighbourhood; and a frog monster lurks in the water and drags under whoever leans too near, the last soul to draw water there having come from the House of Cheap Delights. They also learned that a ruin north-west of the plaza is where the urchins gather.  
**Decision:** Take the stories to the well and see for themselves.  
**Consequence:** The party reached the Piazza dei Rospi with three rumours and no proof.

### Scene 6 — The Well of Frogs

**Location:** Piazza dei Rospi; the Well of Frogs  
**Present:** The party; plaza locals; an old woman with two cats  
**What happened:** The well is ringed by four dog-sized statues of horned bull frogs. It is said to be older than Arakay and cursed. Ed lowered a lantern and saw row after row of frog carvings in the shaft, with no visible bottom. One carving seemed to turn and croak. While Ed was at the well, an old woman read Dagen's palm on the far side of the square.  
**Decision:** Ed withdrew without incident.  
**Consequence:** The well is now a confirmed site of strangeness, not merely a rumor.

### Scene 7 — The palm reading and the Broken Wheel

**Location:** Piazza dei Rospi; Broken Wheel  
**Present:** The party; an old woman with two cats; Nook; Wafik; Rewen  
**What happened:** While Ed was looking down the well, the old woman read Dagen's palm: caverns, death, great reward, and a warning to avoid the green water. The croak from the shaft carried across the square while she was speaking. The party returned to the Broken Wheel and paid 1 gp each for lodging, leaving each purse at 14 gp. Wafik the porter asked whether they were joining the Khosuran armies; Rewen explained how she and Wafik came under Nook's roof.  
**Decision:** Rest and gather local context.  
**Consequence:** Dagen has a prophecy; the party has a lead on the well and a background thread about the Khosuran war.

### Scene 8 — The broken pillar and the Chül

**Location:** A street shrine; a market; the Chül's place  
**Present:** The party; worshippers; a cake seller; the Chül  
**What happened:** The party found people worshipping a broken white pillar carved with a kneeling calf wearing a crown of leaves. Offerings lay at its foot. Elowyn bought a load of sesame cakes with his own gold, and the party learned the way to the Chül. The Chül would buy and sell gear at hard prices. Ed bought a **Fire Rock** from a pile of them for five pieces; it must be thrown down hard on the ground to work, and it is untested. The wares also included a **green rod of glowing metal**, which gave off its own light in daylight and was said to sharpen a spell if chipped; a **silvery fabric pouch**, which looked costly beside his other prices and which nobody asked about; and a **necklace that looked like lapis lazuli** carrying a **cat charm**, priced at 150 gp. Nobody touched any of them.  
**Decision:** Ed spent money on the stone; the rest kept their purses closed.  
**Consequence:** The party has a **Fire Rock**, a new contact, and a clear look at goods none of them can afford.

### Scene 9 — The urchin ruin

**Location:** Ruin north-west of the Piazza dei Rospi  
**Present:** The party; four urchins; Dio  
**What happened:** The party brought cakes to the four urchins. Two came out for the cakes; the oldest, Pablo, arrived with Dio and did the talking. They traded captured crossbows and swords for information. The children confirmed that the water is not safe and that the frog monster is real. They call it **the Splinter**, and it takes children down the well. It was the children who told them that Lady Danarose's son **Orlando** was missing. Elowyn argued that they should seek payment from Lady Danarose before descending. The boy Dio was hired for 1 sp to lead them to her.  
**Decision:** Seek an advance from Lady Danarose before entering the well.  
**Consequence:** The party ended the session approaching Lady Danarose's house with Dio as guide.
