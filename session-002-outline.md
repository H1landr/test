# Session 2, Session Outline

**Working title:** *The Tunnels Under the Shanty*
**Source:** [Session 2 record](session-002.md) and [fact outline](session-002-facts.md)
**Scope:** Twelve scenes, five PCs plus Dio the hireling torchbearer. The session opens at a side door and ends in the altar room.

## What this outline is for

The record says what happened. This page says what each scene must carry, in what order, and whose angle anchors it, so a later pass can retell or check the session without inventing anything.

No prose is written from it. Chapters are retired; the outline is a planning document for the record.

## Spine

1. **The door:** the party takes a missing-person job from a house that despises urchins.
2. **The street:** the guild war and the missing family are both rumours with prices attached.
3. **The descent:** one trapdoor, one store room, one stair, and a room of small skulls.
4. **The dark:** three moths, a lost torch, and a fight in the dark where only Dagen cannot see.
5. **The rooms:** a dead room left alone, a hall of taxidermy, and a trapped altar.
6. **The stop:** ten minutes of rest and no path yet to Orlando.

One angle per scene. All five PCs were present throughout; the anchor is chosen for who had the most at stake or the clearest view, not for attendance.

## Scene outline

### 1. The side door

**Purpose:** Establish the employer, the household's contempt for urchins, and what Dio knows.

- Side door of the Danarosa estate, west of the piazza. A servant answers, not the Lady, and goes to fetch the steward.
- Dio does not beg. Pardoht-Ram is angered by his presence alone, which the party reads as urchins being blamed for Orlando.
- While they wait, Ed asks Dio whether urchins throw their own into the well. Dio says bad men do it, meaning guild men, and is honestly confused by the question.
- Dio adds that a woman from the House of Cheap Delights threw herself in after her child was taken.
- Pardoht-Ram returns: long white beard, H'Zhari in the house, translation offered anyway.

**Turning point:** Elowyn offers condolences and makes the pitch rather than opening with a haggle.

### 2. The courtyard and the terms

**Purpose:** Put a price, a face, and a drawing on the job.

- Courtyard of traditional Arakayan build, false windows facing no street, an estate gone to seed. Water is brought, and a chair for the steward.
- Lady Danarose enters veiled in black, pearls and muted gold medallions. She does not speak to the party directly.
- Terms: Orlando gone 48 hours, enamoured of the urchins; a chest of treasure on his return; 25 gp up front.
- Bozz asks about the sword. Star metal, from the late husband's Khosurian service, carried down by Orlando. The Lady does not want it back.
- Dagen is given Orlando's drawing: a figure with a curved sword, a red-eyed shape opposite, four smaller shapes behind it.

**Turning point:** The party accepts.

### 3. What the Lady knows

**Purpose:** Trade information, not coin. Name the Collector, and show a house that is less offended than expected.

- The Lady knows nothing of the guilds but knows the tunnels: the deeper layers were built by Acheron.
- She has heard of the Collector, a sorcerer who collects wonders and information.
- Anglad asks about rivals. Pardoht-Ram says no group like the party has come, only people after a handout.
- Flatbread is served. All eat at the table; Anglad takes a piece and puts it in his pouch to eat in private, saying plainly that he is ugly. The household seems less offended than expected.

**Turning point:** The party decides to ask the urchins about the Collector before committing.

### 4. The urchins again

**Purpose:** Buy the guide and learn that a guild war is coming.

- Pablo is the oldest and the one who talks. Five cats in the ruin.
- Dio says few of the urchins liked the Collector and none cared about the sword. He is tipped an electrum piece to keep quiet.
- Pablo sells guild knowledge: the Literators keep sewer hideouts, the Barbers are hunting them, a war is coming, and the Barbers would pay for a hideout.

**Turning point:** Five sp buys Dio's company for the day. He becomes guide and torchbearer.

### 5. The House of Cheap Delights

**Purpose:** Turn a rumour into a family.

- The party asks after the woman from the well.
- Busty Rita, dead a week, by her own hand and the well.
- Nian, her daughter, taken on a misty night. She knew to stay inside and did not.
- Hiram, Nian's father, went looking and did not go down the well.
- The house points east to the charcoalers by the broad stairs.

**Turning point:** Follow Hiram's trail, not the well.

### 6. The shrine of Cloacina

**Purpose:** Show a way down that nobody is using, and a goddess nobody tends.

- Domed mud-brick shrine with a stair down to an oval chamber.
- Candles and incense still burning. Two side channels to a 3 ft drain, a statue, and a grated central drain going deep. No attendants.
- Anglad finds no false walls and lights a candle before leaving.

**Turning point:** Leave the drain alone. It stays an open way down.

### 7. The charcoalers

**Purpose:** Buy a drug, place Hiram, and plant a contradiction.

- Desert folk from the southern quarter stand about the broad steps, black-robed.
- A sick man buys charcoal and runs. An intelligence roll establishes charcoal as a drug, breathed in.
- Ed haggles lotus coal from 5 gp to 3 gp and buys.
- A charcoaler says Hiram was an occasional customer and stood at the top of the stairs a couple of nights ago, "not last week". This does not match the house's account. The charcoalers are vague, not lying.

**Turning point:** The party climbs the stairs.

### 8. The shanty and the trapdoor

**Purpose:** Put the entrance somewhere nobody would guard.

- A rundown shanty district around a ruined building. Courtyard choked with weeds and old rubble.
- Dagen finds sign that people have passed through, and a trapdoor used somewhat recently.

**Turning point:** They open it and go down.

### 9. Below the trapdoor

**Purpose:** Establish the undercroft and its wrongness before the fight.

- 10 × 10 stone store room, then a circular stair down about 40 ft.
- 20 × 20 room, better stonework. West doorway, eastern corridor with no end in 30 ft, small skulls piled against the south wall.
- Ed inspects the stone. Elowyn hears rustling behind the door and it stops. Dagen finds tracks of many creatures in the eastern corridor.

**Turning point:** Open the door rather than retreat.

### 10. The moths

**Purpose:** The session's fight, fought blind.

- Two kicks take the door off its frame and end any chance of surprise. Elowyn's arrow is nocked before the first.
- Three grey fuzzy moths come off the ceiling, angry at the light. Ed's thrown dagger hits first for 2.
- One knocks the torch from Dio's hand. The corridor goes dark. Only Dagen lacks infravision.
- Elowyn's arrow misses. Swords after that, Bozz with a mace. Bozz splatters one, Dagen's blade takes another as it impales itself, Elowyn cuts the third in half.
- Dio's torch is relit. Dagen is left without light.

**Turning point:** Fight on in the dark rather than stop to relight.

### 11. The dead room and the domed hall

**Purpose:** Two rooms the party chooses not to touch.

- The south door is where the bloody footprints come from. Beyond it a 15 × 20 ft room with a 6 ft ceiling: ransacked, dried blood pooled at the centre, old campsite traces, the smell of death.
- Dagen reads the prints as a barefoot human's.
- Nobody searches. The room gives them all an uneasy feeling.
- The prints lead to a cross-shaped hall under a 20 ft dome, 15 ft wide and 30 ft deep, full of taxidermied animals, some humanoid, most unfamiliar even to Dagen.

**Turning point:** Leave both rooms and backtrack. The dead room stays unsearched.

### 12. The altar room and the cache

**Purpose:** End on a gain and a needle, not a cliff.

- Short corridor north, a locked standard door 10 ft down. Elowyn picks it.
- 15 × 15 ft room: stone table north, door west, stone font against a wall.
- Ed finds the table is an altar, probes it, and opens a hollow compartment. A poison needle strikes him; he saves and takes no harm.
- The bundle holds five rough-cut amber gems worth 30 gp each and two clay tablets, divine scrolls of Bless and Speak with Animals, unusable by sorcerers. Ed carries both.
- Ten minutes of rest, uninterrupted. Session ends.

**Turning point:** Take the cache and rest rather than push on.

## Fidelity guardrails

- Dio does not beg at the door. A servant answers; Lady Danarose is not there.
- The household speaks H'Zhari, and Pardoht-Ram translates regardless.
- Anglad takes the flatbread and pouches it. He does not refuse it.
- There are **three** moths, and all three die.
- The torch is knocked away and the fight is fought in the dark. Dagen alone lacks infravision.
- The poison needle is harmless on a successful save. Ed then carries the gems and scrolls.
- The dead room is **not searched**. The domed hall is entered, not explored.
- No loot beyond five amber gems and two scrolls. No XP or injury beyond what the record lists.
- Dio is a hireling, not an NPC, and takes no share of exploration XP.
- Nothing is learned about Orlando's whereabouts. The drawing's four shapes go unexplained.
