# Session 2 — The Tunnels Under the Shanty

**Rough notes:** [Session 2 rough notes](session-002-notes.md)  
**One-line summary:** The party takes Lady Danarose's job, walks the district looking for Orlando Danarosa, and goes underground through a ruined shanty courtyard, where they fight moths, find an altar, and stop to rest.  
**Fact outline:** [Session 2 fact outline](session-002-facts.md)  
**Session outline:** [Session 2 session outline](session-002-outline.md)  
**Source confidence:** `high` for events. The session was corrected line by line by Ian on 2026-10-25.  
**Note-taker:** Ian, at the table, roughly.  
**Table:** Five PCs; Keith refereeing; Dio as hireling and torchbearer.

## At the table

- **Players and PCs:** Ian as Elowyn; Sebastian as Anglad the Faceless; Max as Bozz the Well Endowed; Jordan as Dagen; Alex as Illiterate Ed.
- **Referee:** Keith.
- **Hireling:** Dio, urchin torchbearer, carried the torch.
- **Battlemaps:** Not used.

## Summary

- At Lady Danarose's side door the party learns the household's contempt for urchins, and Dio's presence alone angers **Pardoht-Ram**, the steward of House Danarosa. While they wait, Ed gets Dio talking: bad men throw people into the well, not children, and a woman from the House of Cheap Delights drowned herself there after her child was taken.
- Lady Danarose receives them veiled, speaks through **Pardoht-Ram**, and sets terms: 25 gp up front, and a chest of treasure on Orlando's return. Orlando has been gone 48 hours. He took his father's star-metal sword to impress Pablo and went to fight the Splinter.
- The party returns to the urchins. **Pablo**, the oldest, sells them guild knowledge: the Literators keep sewer hideouts, the Barbers are hunting them, and a war is coming. Five sp buys Dio's company for the day.
- At the House of Cheap Delights they put names to the rumour: Rita, her daughter Nian, and Hiram, who went looking for the girl and did not go down the well.
- On the way they pass the shrine of **Cloacina**, goddess of sewers: unattended, a grated drain, candles still burning. At the charcoalers they learn charcoal is a drug, Ed buys lotus coal for 3 gp, and Hiram is placed at the top of the stairs a couple of nights ago.
- Above the stairs, in a ruined shanty courtyard, they find a recently used trapdoor. It opens onto a store room, a 40 ft circular stair, and a 20 × 20 stone room with small skulls piled against the wall.
- Three giant lantern moths come off the ceiling when the door is kicked in. The fight is fought in darkness after Dio's torch is knocked away; all the party but Dagen have infravision. All three moths die.
- A dead room, unsearched, and a domed hall of taxidermy stand empty. In a locked 15 × 15 room they find a stone altar, its compartment trapped with a poison needle. Ed saves, and takes out five rough-cut amber gems and two clay tablets: divine scrolls of Bless and Speak with Animals.
- The party rests ten minutes in the altar room and the session ends there.

## Scene chronology

### Scene 1 — The side door

**Location:** Lady Danarose's house, exterior  
**Present:** The party; Dio; a servant; Pardoht-Ram  
**What happened:** A servant answers the side door and fetches Pardoht-Ram. Dio does not beg; Pardoht-Ram is angered by his presence alone, which the party reads as urchins being blamed for Orlando. While they wait, Ed asks Dio whether urchins really throw one another into the well. Dio says bad men do it, meaning guild men, and is genuinely confused by the question. He adds that a woman from the House of Cheap Delights threw herself in recently after her child went missing. Pardoht-Ram returns: long white beard, H'Zhari spoken in the house, translation offered anyway.  
**Decision:** Elowyn offers condolences and makes the pitch rather than haggling first.  
**Consequence:** Pardoht-Ram accepts their help and invites them into the courtyard.

### Scene 2 — The courtyard and the terms

**Location:** Lady Danarose's courtyard  
**Present:** The party; Lady Danarose; Pardoht-Ram; two guards; servants  
**What happened:** The courtyard shows traditional Arakayan architecture, false windows facing no street, inside an estate gone to seed. Water is brought, and a chair for Pardoht-Ram. Lady Danarose enters veiled in black with pearls and muted gold medallions, and does not speak to the party directly. Pardoht-Ram states the terms: Orlando gone 48 hours, enamoured of the urchins and wanting to join them; the chest of treasure as reward; 25 gp up front. Bozz asks about the sword: star metal, from the late husband's Khosurian service, and Orlando carried it down. Lady Danarose does not want it back and thinks its being there was part of his problem. Dagen is given Orlando's drawing: a figure with a curved sword, a red-eyed shape opposite, four smaller shapes behind it.  
**Decision:** The party accepts the job.  
**Consequence:** They leave with 25 gp, a drawing, and a name to chase.

### Scene 3 — What the Lady knows

**Location:** Lady Danarose's courtyard  
**Present:** The party; Lady Danarose; Pardoht-Ram  
**What happened:** The Lady knows nothing of guilds but knows the tunnels: the deeper layers were built by Acheron. She has heard of the Collector, a sorcerer who collects wonders and information. Pardoht-Ram says no rival group has come, only people after a handout. Flatbread is served; all eat at the table. Anglad takes a piece and puts it in a pouch to eat in private, saying plainly that he is ugly. The household seems less offended than expected.  
**Decision:** The party decides to ask the urchins about the Collector before committing to finding her.  
**Consequence:** The trail leads back to the ruin.

### Scene 4 — The urchins again

**Location:** The Urchin Ruin  
**Present:** The party; Dio; Pablo; the urchins; five cats  
**What happened:** Pablo is the oldest and the one who talks. Dio says few of the urchins liked the Collector and none cared about the sword. Dio is tipped an electrum piece to keep quiet. Pablo says the Literators know of hideouts in the sewers and the Barbers are hunting them, that a guild war is coming, and that the Barbers would pay for a Literator hideout.  
**Decision:** The party pays Pablo 5 sp to take Dio with them.  
**Consequence:** Dio becomes the party's guide and torchbearer underground.

### Scene 5 — The House of Cheap Delights

**Location:** The House of Cheap Delights  
**Present:** The party; the house's people  
**What happened:** The party asks after the woman from the well and is given the family: Busty Rita, dead a week; her daughter Nian, taken on a misty night; her father Hiram, who went looking and did not go down the well. The charcoalers by the stairs to the east may know more.  
**Decision:** Follow Hiram's trail to the charcoalers rather than the well.  
**Consequence:** The party heads east and north.

### Scene 6 — The shrine of Cloacina

**Location:** The Shrine of Cloacina  
**Present:** The party; Dio  
**What happened:** A domed mud-brick shrine with a stair down to an oval chamber. Candles and incense, two side channels running to a 3 ft drain, a statue of the goddess, a grated central drain going deep, and no attendants. Anglad finds no false walls and lights a candle before leaving.  
**Decision:** The party leaves the shrine alone rather than forcing the drain.  
**Consequence:** The shrine stays an open way down.

### Scene 7 — The charcoalers

**Location:** The Charcoalers' Stairs  
**Present:** The party; black-robed dealers; a sick customer  
**What happened:** Desert folk from the southern quarter stand around the broad steps. A sick man buys charcoal and runs. An intelligence roll establishes charcoal as a drug, breathed in. Ed haggles lotus coal from 5 gp to 3 gp. Asked about Hiram, a charcoaler says he was an occasional customer and was at the top of the stairs a couple of nights ago, "not last week", which does not match the house's account. The charcoalers are ambivalent to detail, not deliberately misleading.  
**Decision:** Ed buys the drug; the party climbs the stairs.  
**Consequence:** They reach the shanty district.

### Scene 8 — The shanty and the trapdoor

**Location:** The shanty district above the stairs  
**Present:** The party; Dio; five cats  
**What happened:** A rundown district of shanties around a ruined building. Its courtyard is choked with weeds and rubble and the collapse is old. Dagen finds sign that people have passed through, and a trapdoor used somewhat recently.  
**Decision:** The party opens it and goes down.  
**Consequence:** The party enters the undercroft.

### Scene 9 — Below the trapdoor

**Location:** The Undercroft, store room and 20 × 20 room  
**Present:** The party; Dio with the torch  
**What happened:** A 10 × 10 stone store room, then a circular stair down about 40 ft to a 20 × 20 room with better stonework. West doorway, eastern corridor with no end in 30 ft, and small skulls piled against the south wall. Ed inspects the stone; Elowyn hears rustling behind the door and it stops; Dagen finds tracks of many creatures in the eastern corridor.  
**Decision:** The party opens the door rather than retreating.  
**Consequence:** Combat.

### Scene 10 — The moths

**Location:** The corridor and chamber beyond the door  
**Present:** The party; Dio; three giant lantern moths  
**What happened:** Two kicks take the door off its frame and end any chance of surprise; Elowyn's arrow is nocked before the first. Three grey fuzzy moths come off the ceiling, angry at the light. Ed's thrown dagger hits first for 2. One knocks the torch out of Dio's hand and the corridor goes dark. Only Dagen lacks infravision. Elowyn's arrow misses; the rest of the fight is swords, with Bozz using a mace. Bozz splatters one, Dagen's blade takes another as it impales itself, Elowyn cuts the third in half.  
**Decision:** The party fights on in the dark rather than relighting first.  
**Consequence:** All three moths dead. Dio's torch is relit; Dagen is left without light.

### Scene 11 — The dead room and the domed hall

**Location:** The Undercroft, south door and domed hall  
**Present:** The party  
**What happened:** The south door is where the bloody footprints come from. Beyond it, a 15 × 20 ft room with a 6 ft ceiling: ransacked, dried blood pooled at the centre, old campsite traces along the walls, and the smell of death. Dagen reads the prints as a barefoot human's. The party does not search it; the room gives them all an uneasy feeling. The prints lead to a cross-shaped hall with a 20 ft dome, 15 ft wide and 30 ft deep, full of taxidermied animals, some humanoid, most unfamiliar even to Dagen.  
**Decision:** Leave both rooms alone and backtrack.  
**Consequence:** The dead room stays unsearched.

### Scene 12 — The altar room and the cache

**Location:** The Undercroft, altar room  
**Present:** The party; Dio  
**What happened:** At the end of a short corridor to the north, a locked standard door 10 ft down. Elowyn picks it. Inside, 15 × 15 ft: stone table north, door west, stone font against a wall. Ed finds the table is an altar, probes it, and opens a hollow compartment; a poison needle strikes him and he saves, and it does him no harm. The bundle holds five rough-cut amber gems worth 30 gp each and two clay tablets, divine scrolls of Bless and Speak with Animals, unusable by sorcerers. Ed carries both. The party rests ten minutes without interruption.  
**Decision:** Take the cache and rest rather than push on.  
**Consequence:** Session ends in the altar room.
