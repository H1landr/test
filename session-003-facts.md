## Events in order

### 1. The altar room and the visitor

- The session opens where Session 2 ends: the party resting in the altar room, room 3 on the map, with Dio.
- Fluttering against the west door, scuttling near the bottom of the door, then the fluttering stops.
- Someone tries the south door. That is the room's entry door, the one the party came in by, on the corridor north of the dead room. Ed listens and hears what sounds like barefoot footfalls.
- The party is captivated by the curious sound of someone trying the door and walking around barefoot.
- Plan: bait the stranger back inside and jump him if it turns dangerous. Dio is chosen to call out.
- Looking down the corridor, Anglad sees a dirty, bearded man peering round the corner. A wild-man type.
- The party offers safety. He does not believe it and leaves.

### 2. The plan for the moths

- The party opens the west door, into room 5, and leaves Dio in the corner of the altar room with the lantern, to bait the moths in.
- Beyond the door is the eight-column hall. The moths are disturbed; there seem to be more than five inside.
- New plan: light and throw a torch in, then an oil flask once the moths flock to the torch.

### 3. The moth fight

- Surprise round (round 0): Elowyn and Ed throw oil flasks. Elowyn's misses the moths and hits a pillar. Ed's hits four moths for 2 damage each; two are incinerated at once.
- Round 1, the party wins initiative: three missile attacks miss. Two moths burst into flame. Bozz and Anglad block the door.
- Round 2, the party loses initiative, the moths are still busy: Ed, Bozz, Dagen and Anglad enter the room. Elowyn stays behind and puts an arrow through a very thin wing. Anglad skewers a moth cleanly. Ed, Dagen and Bozz miss.
- Round 3: Elowyn charges, smacks a moth and finishes it. Bozz misses. Ed hits for 2. Dagen hits and kills it.
- Victory. There were seven moths and all seven died.

### 4. The pillar room

- The room is still on fire. Furniture is smouldering. Eight marble pillars hold up the ceiling of a room that was once finer than it is now.
- 20 ft wide, 25 ft long.
- Dio had hidden behind the altar when he baited the moths. He rejoined the party after the fight and carried the lantern.
- Bozz and Ed search. They find clothing, equipment and a pack.
- Bozz finds a larva about a foot long and squashes it.
- Found: 76 cp, 38 sp, a suit of leather armour, a sling with 20 silver bullets, a studded wood club, a pointy yellow cap (Ed puts it on at once), a flask of clear water, and a small leather bag of colourful ribbons and pearl-encrusted buttons.
- The party leaves by the only other door in the room, into a hallway that turns south after 15 ft.
- Dagen puts out what is left of the fire.

### 5. The hallway and the stair

- Elowyn finds no traps in the hallway.
- No wandering monsters.
- Round the corner the hallway turns west and ends in a steep staircase up.
- A sign at the top reads "knock first" in the trade tongue.
- Shuffling on the other side. A voice asks how they got past the moths. Anglad answers.

### 6. The Archivist

- The party comes in by the eastern door, under the "knock first" sign. The chamber is room 6 on the map.
- The door opens on a man in well-kept robes, dark skin, neat beard.
- His room is well lit and full of books, parchments and shelves, with reading stands and texts of every kind.
- Long and narrow: 15 ft wide, 25 ft long, with doors at the far sides and one large door at the far end.
- The door opposite the entrance, the western one, leads to the tunnels to the well.
- He gives the party a crudely drawn map of the area. It is missing details: no dead room (called the murder room in the notes), and the pillar room is drawn small.
- He knows of the Collector: the entrance to her hall is below the Barking Dogs tavern.
- He is a Kessian bookkeeper, and is known as the Archivist. His name is Mnarka.
- Ed asks about spellbooks. Mnarka scuttles about and trades the tablets for a spellbook. The book holds six arcane spells: Floating Disc, Light, Protection from Evil, Read Languages, Invisibility, and Web. Ed is the only member of the party who can use it.
- The tablets are the two clay tablets from Session 2, the divine scrolls of Bless and Speak with Animals. A trade counts as a sale, so their 250 XP each still applies.
- A small paper figure of a horse jumps out of the spellbook.
- The origami creatures are called the paperkin, and stay to one shelf.
- `[?]` Whether the paperkin lives in the spellbook, on the shelf, or both.
- Mnarka will reward the party with knowledge if they find out who was trying to dig into the room. The party agreed to come back once they were done finding the kids.

### 7. The rest in the bedroom

- The party finishes its rest in the bedroom. Six of them sleep there, the Archivist included.
- Ed inspects the room and finds more books.

### 8. Questions for the Archivist

- He does not know where the animals in the domed hall came from.
- He does not know who the wild man is.
- The skulls are said to be from "those hooked on the coal willing to do dark deeds".
- He leads the party to the western door, the one toward the well of frogs.
- He explains that the well of frogs houses evil, previously worshipped by Acheron.

### 9. Down the spiral stair

- The western door leads to a passage with another spiral staircase, 20 ft down, ending in an unlocked door.
- Beyond it: a door leading south, a hallway leading southwest, a collapsed tunnel to the north, and to the west a man sitting against the wall with a candleholder beside him.
- The room is room 7 on the map.
- The Amnesiac remembers nothing and loves counting.
- Ed tries ventriloquism to get him to give up the stick. It does not work. The man uses the stick on Ed.

### 10. The Amnesiac

- The Amnesiac wins initiative.
- He uses the stick on Ed. It shoots a bolt for 2 damage.
- Bozz and Anglad miss. Dagen slashes his throat open.
- Victory.

### 11. After the Amnesiac

- On him and beside him: 8 candles, a candleholder, a leather medallion bearing a candle, and a wand of magic missile. The stick he used is that wand; it was first seen as a stick before it was used, its charges are unknown, and its command word is "Shras!".
- Dagen picks the mushrooms and identifies them as Candlecap mushrooms. Enough of them make a paste for fire resistance.
- The remaining door is blocked. It is unlocked, but something is pressed against it, and it does not look as though anyone has tried to force it shut. It is the south door of room 7.
- Anglad inspects the northern tunnel. It seems normal, just collapsed.
- The session ends here.
