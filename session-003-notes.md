- We are resting in the altar room. We hear fluttering against the Western door. We can hear scuttling near the bottom of the door, then the fluttering stops. 
- We hear someone try the southern door. Ed listens at the door and hears what sounds like barefoot footfalls. We are enraptured. 
- We decide to bait them back inside, and jump them if things get hairy. Dio is chosen to call out to the stranger. 
- Looking down the corridor, Anglad sees a dirty bearded man peering around the corner. Wild man type. 
- We offer safety, he's not dumb enough to believe us and leaves. 
- We decide to open the Western door, and leave Dio in the corner of the room with the lantern to bait the moths inside. 
- We open the door - the other room is quite large, with several pillars. The moths seem disturbed, there seem to be more than 5 inside. 
- New plan - we light and throw a torch into the room, then an oil flask once the moths flock to the torch.

- On opening the door, we see the man better. He's in well kept robes, dark skin, neat beard.

- The man knows of the collector - the entrance to her hall is below the barking dogs tavern.

- Ed asks about spellbooks - The Archivist scuttles about, and trades the tablets for a spellbook. A small, paper figure of a horse jumps out of the spellbook. 
- The origami creatures are collectively known as 'the paper king', and stay to one shelf. 
- Manarko - The archivist - is happy to reward us with knowledge if we can find out who was trying to dig into the room.

- The Archivist leads us to the other door toward the frogs. Explains the well of frogs houses evil, previously worshipped by the previous empire (arakavian??)
